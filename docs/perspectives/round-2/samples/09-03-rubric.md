## Scoring Rubric

Each criterion is scored at four levels. Circle or record the level that best matches the performance, then enter the points for that level in the Score column.

| Criterion | Level 4: Excellent (4 points) | Level 3: Proficient (3 points) | Level 2: Developing (2 points) | Level 1: Beginning (1 point) | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tone quality | Tone is full, steady and resonant across the whole range. Sound is clear with no unplanned breathiness, buzzing, pinching or thinness. Tone stays consistent when moving between registers and dynamics. | Tone is mostly full and steady. One or two brief moments of thinness, breathiness or unfocused sound, usually in the highest or lowest notes. | Tone is often thin, breathy, pinched or inconsistent. The sound changes noticeably between notes or phrases, and the performer seems to be managing tone rather than producing it easily. | Tone is frequently unfocused, strained or hard to hear. Many notes lack a clear center, or the sound is interrupted by air leaks, cracks or squeaks. | ____ / 4 |
| Intonation | Nearly all pitches are in tune with the ensemble and the reference pitch. Performer adjusts quickly when a note drifts, and chords and unison lines blend. | Most pitches are in tune. A few notes are slightly sharp or flat, and the performer corrects most of them. Blend is mostly good. | Several notes are noticeably sharp or flat, especially on long tones, leaps or cadences. Performer rarely adjusts, and the section sometimes sounds out of tune with others. | Pitches are frequently inaccurate. Many notes are clearly sharp or flat, and the performer does not hear or correct the problem. The part stands out as out of tune. | ____ / 4 |
| Rhythmic accuracy | All notes and rests are played or sung at the correct length and entrance. The performer stays with the steady beat and the ensemble, including through syncopation, ties and tempo changes. | Most rhythms are accurate. One to three minor errors, such as a rushed dotted rhythm or a late entrance, that the performer recovers from quickly. | Rhythm is often inaccurate. Several notes or rests are the wrong length, the tempo drifts, or the performer enters late or early more than once. | Rhythm is frequently incorrect or unsteady. The performer often loses the beat, skips measures or cannot rejoin the ensemble without help. | ____ / 4 |
| Dynamics and expression | Performs the full range of written dynamics (pp to ff), with clear crescendos and decrescendos. Phrasing, articulations (staccato, legato, accents) and expression markings are clearly shown and match the musical style. | Shows most written dynamics and articulations. Crescendos and decrescendos are present but may be less gradual or less wide. Phrasing is mostly clear. | Dynamics are limited to a narrow range, often loud or soft throughout. Some articulations or expression markings are missed or applied the same way every time. Phrases are unclear. | Dynamics and articulations are mostly ignored or applied at random. The performance sounds flat, with no sense of shape or phrasing. | ____ / 4 |
| Stage presence | Enters and exits with confidence and poise. Posture is upright and relaxed, and the performer keeps a focused, engaged expression throughout. Responds to the conductor's cues promptly and maintains good instrument or singing posture. | Enters, exits and stands with mostly good posture and focus. Occasional visual distraction, such as looking down too long or shifting weight, but the performer stays engaged. | Posture or focus breaks down at times. The performer looks down or away often, fidgets, slouches, or responds slowly to cues. Entrances or exits are hesitant. | Performer appears disengaged or uncomfortable. Posture is consistently slumped or tense, eye contact is absent, and cues are frequently missed or ignored. | ____ / 4 |

## Student Self-Check Checklist

Before and after your performance, check off what you did:

- [ ] I played or sang my notes in tune with the reference pitch and adjusted when I heard a problem.
- [ ] My tone was full and steady from the lowest to the highest note.
- [ ] I counted and stayed with the beat, including tricky rhythms and rests.
- [ ] I entered on time and rejoined the group if I lost my place.
- [ ] I played or sang soft and loud parts as written and shaped my phrases.
- [ ] I used the articulations (staccato, legato, accents) marked in my music.
- [ ] I stood or sat with good posture and kept a focused expression.
- [ ] I watched the conductor and responded to cues right away.
- [ ] I entered and exited the stage with confidence.
