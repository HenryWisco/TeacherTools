## Vocabulary List

### English

| Word | Part of speech | Student-friendly definition | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| root | noun | The part of a plant under the ground. It holds the plant and drinks water. | The root holds the plant in the soil. |
| stem | noun | The part that holds up the leaves and flower. | The stem is tall and green. |
| leaf | noun | The flat green part that makes food for the plant. | The leaf catches the sunlight. |
| flower | noun | The colorful part of a plant that can make seeds. | The flower is red. |
| seed | noun | A small part that can grow into a new plant. | I planted a seed in the dirt. |
| water | noun | A clear liquid that plants need to grow. | The plant needs water. |
| sunlight | noun | Light from the sun. Plants need it to make food. | Plants grow in sunlight. |
| soil | noun | The brown or dark ground where plants grow. | The seed is in the soil. |
| air | noun | The gas around us. Plants use it to make food. | Plants need air and light. |
| grow | verb | To get bigger over time. | The plant will grow tall. |

| agua | sustantivo | Un líquido claro que las plantas necesitan para crecer. | La planta necesita agua. |
