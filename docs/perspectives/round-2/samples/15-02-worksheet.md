# Adding and Subtracting Within 100 with Regrouping

**Note to Families**

Dear Family,

This worksheet gives your child practice adding and subtracting two-digit numbers, including regrouping (also called "carrying" when adding and "borrowing" when subtracting). Your child has been learning to break numbers into tens and ones. Here is how you can help at home:

- Ask your child to show each step with objects, such as coins, beans, or drawings of tens sticks and single cubes.
- Encourage your child to say the place value out loud: "4 ones plus 7 ones is 11 ones, so that is 1 ten and 1 one."
- Praise effort and careful thinking more than speed. Mistakes are a normal part of learning.
- If your child gets stuck, read the problem together and ask, "Do we have enough ones to take away?" or "Did we make a new ten?"

Thank you for supporting your child's learning!

**Worked Example (Adding with regrouping):**

  47
+ 25
----
  72

Step 1: 7 ones + 5 ones = 12 ones. Write 2 in the ones place and regroup 1 ten.
Step 2: 4 tens + 2 tens + 1 ten (regrouped) = 7 tens.
Answer: 72

**Worked Example (Subtracting with regrouping):**

  52
- 17
----
  35

Step 1: 2 ones is not enough to take away 7 ones. Regroup 1 ten as 10 ones. Now you have 12 ones.
Step 2: 12 ones - 7 ones = 5 ones.
Step 3: 4 tens - 1 ten = 3 tens.
Answer: 35
