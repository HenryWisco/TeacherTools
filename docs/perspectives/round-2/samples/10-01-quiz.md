## Answer Key

**Part A: Multiple Choice (1 point each, total 10 points)**

1. C. Shade 10 to 12. Stick welding is typically rated shade 10 to 12 depending on amperage. (1 point)
2. D. Shade 12 to 14. Oxy-acetylene cutting of thick plate, and some torch work, calls for a darker filter; ANSI Z49.1 and common shade charts list shade 4 to 8 for torch cutting and brazing. Shade 12 to 14 is more typical for high-intensity arcs such as plasma or carbon arc. (1 point)

   Note to instructor: Because torch cutting shade charts vary, accept A or C only if the instructor's shop lens chart supports it; the answer key keys D as the best answer for the arc-level and heavy-cutting context used in this course. Verify against your current ANSI Z49.1 table before administering.

3. C. Acetylene. Acetylene cylinders must be kept upright to keep acetone from escaping with the gas. (1 point)
4. B. 15 psig, which is about 1.0 bar gauge. Acetylene should not be used above 15 psig (1.0 bar) because it can decompose explosively above this pressure. (1 point)
5. B. Liquid acetone can flow out and damage the regulator and cause unstable flow. (1 point)
6. B. A welding helmet with the correct lens shade, flame-resistant jacket, leather gloves, and leather boots. (1 point)
7. B. Close the cylinder valve, release the pressure in the regulator, and report the leak to the instructor. (1 point)
8. C. Local exhaust ventilation with a captured hood or fume extractor placed close to the arc. Source capture is the most effective control. (1 point)
9. C. At least 30 minutes. OSHA 1910.252 and NFPA 51B call for fire watch for at least 30 minutes after hot work ends. (1 point)
10. B. They prevent the flame from traveling back into the hoses and cylinders. (1 point)

**Part B: Short Answer (3 points each, total 9 points)**

11. Any three of: oxygen can accelerate fire and combustion of oil or grease (violent reaction); a fire or leak at one cylinder can spread to the other; oxygen with grease or oil can ignite or explode; a fall or heat can rupture a cylinder; gas cylinders stored together create a fire hazard; or stored acetylene and oxygen must be separated by at least 20 feet or a 5-foot fire-rated barrier (per OSHA 1910.253). Award 1 point per correct hazard, maximum 3.
12. Cylinders must be secured so they cannot fall, which can break the valve and release gas violently. They should be placed upright and away from heat, sparks, and the work area, with the valve protection cap on when not in use. Award 1.5 points for each part (securing and placement).
13. Any three of: check hoses for cracks, cuts, or leaks; verify regulators and gauges are working; confirm the torch tip is clean and correct for the task; check that flashback arrestors are installed; check connections are tight; verify the work area is clear of combustibles; confirm ventilation and fire extinguisher are present. Award 1 point per correct item, maximum 3.

**Part C: Application and Reasoning (5 points each, total 10 points)**

14. Award up to 5 points. Expected unsafe conditions and corrections:
    - Sparks reaching the wooden workbench: move the work, or shield the combustibles with fire-resistant blankets or screens; a 35-foot clearance is the common guideline for combustibles.
    - Rags near the work area: remove the rags to a closed metal container or a safe distance, since oily rags can spontaneously combust.
    - Corner placement with no clear exit: move to an open area with proper ventilation and a clear escape path.
    - Fire extinguisher and fire watch not evident: a fire extinguisher must be within reach and a fire watch must be assigned.
    Award 1 to 1.5 points per condition identified and corrected, up to 5 total.

15. Award up to 5 points. Expected content:
    - A shade 9 lens is too light for SMAW on mild steel, which needs shade 10 to 12 for typical amperages; it lets too much harmful UV and infrared radiation through and the arc is too bright to view safely (1.5 points).
    - The correct range is shade 10 to 12 (1 point).
    - Two symptoms of arc eye (photokeratitis): gritty or sandy feeling in the eyes, watery or red eyes, headache, sensitivity to light, or temporary vision blurriness appearing hours after exposure. Award 1 point each, maximum 2.5 points.
