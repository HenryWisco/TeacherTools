## Activity 1: Warm-up (0:00 to 0:05)

**Read aloud to the class:**

"Good morning. Today your teacher wants you to review balancing chemical equations. You already know the basics. Today is about practice. When you finish a problem, check that you have the same number of each type of atom on both sides of the arrow. Let's begin."

Take attendance using [attendance method].

## Activity 2: Guided Review (0:05 to 0:15)

**Read aloud to the class:**

"A chemical equation shows reactants on the left and products on the right. The arrow means 'yields.' To balance an equation, you change the coefficients, which are the numbers in front of the formulas. You never change the subscripts, which are the small numbers inside the formulas. Changing subscripts would change the substance itself."

**Steps to read or write on the board:**
1. Write the unbalanced equation.
2. Count the atoms of each element on the left side.
3. Count the atoms of each element on the right side.
4. Change the coefficients to make the counts match.
5. Check every element again.

**Work this example with the class:**

Example: H2 + O2 → H2O

"Count the atoms. On the left there are 2 hydrogen atoms and 2 oxygen atoms. On the right there are 2 hydrogen atoms and 1 oxygen atom. Oxygen does not match. Put a 2 in front of H2O. Now the right side has 4 hydrogen atoms and 2 oxygen atoms. Hydrogen does not match. Put a 2 in front of H2 on the left. Now both sides have 4 hydrogen atoms and 2 oxygen atoms. The equation is balanced: 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O."

**Ask the class:** "What would happen if we changed the subscript in H2O to H2O2?" Let students answer. Then say: "That would be a different substance, hydrogen peroxide. We cannot change subscripts."

## Activity 3: Independent Practice (0:15 to 0:35)

Hand out Student Sheet A. Read this direction aloud:

"Work on Part 1 and Part 2 by yourself. Show your counting for each problem. When you finish, check every element one more time."

Walk around the room. Look at the work, not just the final answer. If a student is stuck, point to the step where counting begins and say, "Start by counting the atoms on each side."

## Activity 4: Partner Check and Challenge (0:35 to 0:45)

**Read aloud to the class:**

"Turn to a partner. Compare your answers to Part 2 on Student Sheet A. If your answers do not match, talk through your counting until you agree. Then start Student Sheet B, Part 3."

Give the class about five minutes for partner checks. Then have them work on Part 3 for the remaining time.

## Activity 5: Collect and Dismiss (0:45 to 0:50)

**Read aloud to the class:**

"Please put your Student Sheet A and Student Sheet B on your desk. I will collect them. Thank you for your work today. Your teacher will check your sheets when they return."

Collect the sheets. Stack Sheet A and Sheet B separately. Place them in [location for collected work]. Dismiss students according to [dismissal procedure].
