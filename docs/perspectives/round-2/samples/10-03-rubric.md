# Performance Rubric: SMAW Fillet Weld, 3/8 in. Mild Steel T-Joint, 2F Position

**Name:** ______________________  **Date:** ____________

## Assignment Overview

Students weld a 3/8 in. mild steel T-joint fillet weld in the horizontal (2F) position using a 1/8 in. E7018 electrode. The test is scored on six criteria. Total possible points: 24.

**Safety is pass/fail.** Any safety violation stops the test immediately and the score is zero. Safety violations include: missing or improper PPE (helmet with correct shade lens, flame-resistant jacket or sleeves, gloves, safety glasses under the helmet, leather boots), an unsecured or damaged electrode holder or ground clamp, or any unsafe action near flammable material or other people. The instructor may stop the test at any time for a safety concern.

## Scoring Table

Point values appear in each column header. Points are awarded for each criterion using the level that best matches the observed work. Scores may fall between levels only if the instructor records a whole-number score that matches a level description.

| Criterion | Level 4 (highest) | Level 3 | Level 2 | Level 1 (lowest) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Safety and PPE** (Pass/Fail gate; if failed, total score = 0) | **Pass:** Full PPE worn and maintained for the entire test: helmet with correct shade lens, flame-resistant jacket, gloves, leather boots, safety glasses under helmet. Stinger and ground clamp in safe condition. No hazards created. Points: 4 | Not applicable. Any PPE or safety violation results in a fail. Points: 0 | Not applicable. Points: 0 | Not applicable. Points: 0 |
| **Machine Setup** (4 pts) | **4 pts:** Correct polarity for E7018 (DC electrode positive, reverse polarity). Amperage set within the electrode range for 1/8 in. E7018 (about 110 to 140 A) and verified. Ground clamp secured to clean metal. Electrode dried and in use, not contaminated. Setup completed without prompting. | **3 pts:** Polarity or amperage is correct, but one item is missing or needs one prompt to correct. Ground secure. Electrode in good condition. | **2 pts:** Two setup items are incorrect or require repeated prompting, such as amperage outside range or a loose or poorly placed ground. Electrode condition is questionable. | **1 pt:** Polarity wrong or amperage set well outside range, or the machine is not set up before the test starts. Work cannot begin safely without rework. |
| **Bead Appearance and Profile** (6 pts) | **6 pts:** Uniform bead width and ripple pattern with consistent spacing along the full length. Smooth transitions at the start and stop. Slag fully removed without gouging the bead. Bead is slightly convex or flat, with toes blending smoothly into the base metal. | **4 to 5 pts:** Mostly uniform bead. Minor variation in ripple or width in one section. Smooth overall, with one start or stop that is slightly abrupt. Profile is acceptable with minor irregularity. | **2 to 3 pts:** Noticeable variation in width or ripple across the length. Several abrupt starts or stops. Bead is noticeably over- or under-convex in places. Slag is partly left on the bead. | **0 to 1 pts:** Erratic bead width and ripple. Bead is ropy, highly convex, or flat with poor toe blending. Heavy slag left on bead or bead gouged during cleaning. |
| **Weld Size (Leg Size)** (6 pts) | **6 pts:** Both legs measure 3/8 in. (within plus or minus 1/16 in.), checked with a fillet weld gauge. Equal legs. Weld covers the full joint length. | **4 to 5 pts:** Legs measure within plus or minus 1/8 in. of the 3/8 in. target. Minor leg inequality (no more than 1/16 in. difference). Full joint length covered. | **2 to 3 pts:** Legs measure 1/4 in. to 5/16 in. or 7/16 in. to 1/2 in. Noticeable unequal legs. Weld covers the joint but has a short section of reduced size. | **0 to 1 pts:** Legs under 1/4 in. or over 1/2 in., or weld does not cover the full joint length. Large unequal legs. |
| **Defects: Undercut, Porosity, Overlap** (6 pts) | **6 pts:** No undercut deeper than 1/32 in. and no more than 2 in. total length in any 12 in. section. No porosity visible. No overlap or cold lap at the toes. Visual inspection meets AWS D1.1 Table 6.1 acceptance criteria for statically loaded nontubular connections. | **4 to 5 pts:** Undercut 1/32 in. to 1/16 in. in limited length, meeting the AWS D1.1 limit for the joint. Isolated surface porosity (no more than 1 pore, 1/32 in. or smaller). No overlap. | **2 to 3 pts:** Undercut deeper than 1/16 in. or extended over a length that exceeds the AWS D1.1 limit. Visible porosity (more than 1 pore). Minor overlap on one toe. | **0 to 1 pts:** Multiple defects: undercut exceeding the AWS D1.1 limit at several points, significant porosity, overlap along a long section, or crack visible on the surface. |
| **Cleanup** (2 pts) | **2 pts:** All slag removed with chipping hammer and wire brush. Stinger and ground clamp hung properly. Work area left free of spatter, tools put away, and consumables (electrode stubs) disposed of correctly. | **1 pt:** Minor cleanup missed, such as a small amount of slag or a few spatter spots. Tools and cables put away with one reminder. | **0 pts:** Significant slag or spatter left. Cables or tools left in an unsafe position or not returned. | **0 pts:** Work area left hazardous or unsafe. (Treat as a safety concern and follow the Safety section.) |

*Note on Cleanup and Safety: an unsafe cleanup action, such as leaving a hot electrode stub or cable in a walkway, is a safety violation and results in a fail under the Safety gate.*

## Student Checklist

Before you start, check these:
- [ ] Helmet, jacket, gloves, boots, and glasses on and in good condition
- [ ] Polarity set correctly for E7018 (DC electrode positive)
- [ ] Amperage set in range for 1/8 in. E7018
- [ ] Ground clamp on clean metal
- [ ] Electrode is dry and in good condition

While you weld, check these:
- [ ] Bead width and ripples are even along the whole joint
- [ ] Starts and stops blend smoothly
- [ ] Both legs are about 3/8 in. and equal
- [ ] No deep undercut, pores, or overlap at the toes

When you finish, check these:
- [ ] Slag is removed without gouging the bead
- [ ] Spatter cleaned up, stub disposed of safely
- [ ] Stinger and ground clamp hung properly
- [ ] Tools and work area left clean and safe
