## Answer Key

1. C. Density is mass per unit of volume. (1 point)
2. C. Grams per milliliter (g/mL) is the common unit for liquid density. (1 point)
3. B. Density is a property of the material and does not depend on the size of the sample. (1 point)
4. Density = 40 g / 10 cm^3 = 4 g/cm^3. (3 points; 1 point for correct formula, 1 for correct division, 1 for units)
5. Density = 54 g / 6 cm^3 = 9 g/cm^3. (3 points; 1 point for correct formula, 1 for correct division, 1 for units)
6. Volume of metal = 30 mL - 20 mL = 10 mL (which equals 10 cm^3). Density = 84 g / 10 mL = 8.4 g/mL. (4 points; 1 point for finding the volume by subtraction, 1 for the correct formula, 1 for correct division, 1 for units)
7. Density = 25 g / 20 mL = 1.25 g/mL. (3 points; 1 point for correct formula, 1 for correct division, 1 for units)
8. A. Object X has a mass of 30 g and Object Y has a mass of 60 g with equal volumes, so Object X has the lower density. Correction note: Object Y is denser, so the correct answer is B. Object Y has a greater density than Object X. (1 point)
9. B. Because 0.6 g/cm^3 is less than 1.0 g/cm^3, the wood will float. (1 point)
10. Sample answer: An object with a density greater than water has more mass packed into each unit of volume than the same volume of water. Because it is denser than water, gravity pulls it down more strongly than the water pushes it up, so it sinks.
