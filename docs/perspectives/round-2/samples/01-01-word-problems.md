## Answer Key

1. Multiply 12 × 24. 12 × 20 = 240 and 12 × 4 = 48. 240 + 48 = 288. Answer: There are 288 bookmarks in all.

2. Multiply 3 × 6 = 18 dollars spent. Subtract from 20: 20 − 18 = 2. Answer: Amara gets 2 dollars in change.

3. Divide 45 ÷ 4. 4 × 11 = 44, with 1 left over. Answer: The teacher can make 11 full teams, and 1 student is left over. (Remainder interpreted by dropping it for the number of full teams and reporting the leftover student separately.)

4. Divide 50 ÷ 6. 6 × 8 = 48, with 2 left over. Answer: Each classroom gets 8 stickers, and 2 stickers are left over.

5. Divide 40 ÷ 7. 7 × 5 = 35, with 5 left over. Answer: Luis can buy 5 books, and he has 5 dollars left. (Remainder interpreted as money left over.)

6. Multiply 14 × 23. 14 × 20 = 280 and 14 × 3 = 42. 280 + 42 = 322. Add 38 extra books: 322 + 38 = 360. Answer: There are 360 books in all.

7. Step 1: Find the total cost of the books. 29 × 8 = 232 dollars. (Note: 29 × 8 = 20 × 8 + 9 × 8 = 160 + 72 = 232.)
Step 2: Divide the books into bags. The number of books is 29 students, so there are 29 books. Divide 29 ÷ 4. 4 × 7 = 28, with 1 left over. Answer: The class needs 8 bags. The last bag holds 1 book. (Remainder interpreted by rounding up, since every book needs a bag.)

8. Step 1: Find the packs needed. 36 students need 36 bags, and each pack holds 6 bags. 36 ÷ 6 = 6, with no remainder. So 6 packs are needed.
Step 2: Find the cost. 6 packs × 5 dollars = 30 dollars.
Step 3: Find the money left. The school has 25 dollars, which is not enough for 30 dollars. Answer: The school needs 6 packs, but it has only 25 dollars, so it cannot afford all 6 packs at 5 dollars each. (Note for teacher: as written, the cost is 30 dollars and the budget is 25 dollars. Teachers may wish to change the budget to 30 dollars or more, in which case the money left is 0 dollars if the budget is 30 dollars.)
