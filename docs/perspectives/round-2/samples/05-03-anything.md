# One-Week Kindergarten Literacy and Math Centers: Letters m, s, t, a and Counting to 10

**Name:** ______________________  **Date:** ____________

## Plan Overview

- **Length:** 5 days (Monday to Friday)
- **Centers:** 4 per day, 15 minutes each
- **Group size:** 5 or 6 students per group (4 groups per class of 20 to 24)
- **Focus:** letter sounds /m/, /s/, /t/, /a/ and counting to 10
- **Materials to print:** the picture direction cards in this document (one per center, laminate if possible)

### Rotation Schedule

Groups rotate clockwise through the four centers. Each group visits every center once during the week. Use the same rotation all five days, or shift the starting center each day so every group visits every center at different times.

| Group | Center 1 (Minutes 0 to 15) | Center 2 (Minutes 15 to 30) | Center 3 (Minutes 30 to 45) | Center 4 (Minutes 45 to 60) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Group A (Sun) | Sound Sort | Sound Hunt | Counting Trays | Name Stamp |
| Group B (Star) | Counting Trays | Sound Sort | Name Stamp | Sound Hunt |
| Group C (Ladybug) | Sound Hunt | Name Stamp | Sound Sort | Counting Trays |
| Group D (Fish) | Name Stamp | Counting Trays | Sound Hunt | Sound Sort |

Post this chart with the group symbols. Students find their symbol and follow the line across the row to see where to go next.

### Center Summary

| Center | Skill | Who Works Independently | Adult Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sound Sort | Matching letter sounds to pictures | Yes | Check first round |
| Sound Hunt | Finding /m/, /s/, /t/, /a/ objects | Yes | None during center |
| Counting Trays | Counting objects to 10 | Yes | Check first round |
| Name Stamp | Letter recognition and sound in own name | Yes | Support as needed |

## Prep for the Whole Week

Gather these materials before Monday:

1. Four picture cards per letter sound: a picture of an object starting with /m/ (for example mop, mouse, moon, mitten), /s/ (sun, sock, spoon, snake), /t/ (top, tent, turtle, truck), and /a/ (apple, ant, ax, alligator). Use real photos or clear drawings. Total: 16 picture cards.
2. A set of 4 letter cards: m, s, t, a (large, 4 inches or larger).
3. A bin of small objects that start with /m/, /s/, /t/, /a/ (toy mouse, toy sun, toy turtle, plastic apple, and so on). Include about 3 objects per sound, 12 total, plus 6 distractor objects that do not start with these sounds (for example a block, a cup, a button).
4. Sorting mats: 4 paper plates or sheets labeled with the letter m, s, t, a.
5. Counting trays: 4 trays or paper plates. Put 10 identical small objects in each tray (dried beans, cubes, or buttons), with a number card 1 to 10 for checking. Make 4 trays with different objects so students do not all count the same thing.
6. Name stamp setup: a stamp pad or dot markers, a class list of first names (write each child's name on a card with the first letter in a different color). Use placeholders like [Student Name] on your own list if needed. Keep a few blank strips of paper.
7. Dry-erase boards or paper, pencils, crayons.
8. Print the four direction cards on the following pages (one per center), and post each at its center.

## Center 1: Sound Sort

**Skill:** Match a picture to the letter that makes its beginning sound.

### Picture Direction Card (print and post)

[Picture: a card showing a letter m mat with a mop, then a letter s mat with a sun, then a letter t mat with a turtle, then a letter a mat with an apple. Arrow from a picture card to a mat.]

**Step 1:** Take a picture card.
**Step 2:** Say the name of the picture slowly: "mmmm-op."
**Step 3:** Listen for the first sound.
**Step 4:** Put the card on the mat with that letter.

### What Students Do

1. Pick up one picture card from the basket.
2. Say the picture name slowly and stretch the first sound.
3. Place it on the matching letter mat (m, s, t, or a).
4. Repeat until all 16 cards are sorted or 15 minutes end.

### Teacher Prep

- Make 16 picture cards as described above.
- Lay out 4 mats with letters m, s, t, a in a row.
- Put all picture cards in a basket.

### Teacher Check

Sit with the group for the first round. Ask: "What sound do you hear at the beginning?" Confirm the placement with a thumbs-up or a gentle redirect.

## Center 2: Sound Hunt

**Skill:** Find objects that start with a target sound.

### Picture Direction Card (print and post)

[Picture: a child's hand reaching into a bin, pulling out a toy turtle, then placing it in a tray beside a large letter t card.]

**Step 1:** Look at the letter card on the table.
**Step 2:** Say its sound.
**Step 3:** Find objects in the bin that start with that sound.
**Step 4:** Put them in the tray.

### What Students Do

1. Look at the letter card (m, s, t, or a).
2. Say its sound (for example /t/).
3. Pull out objects that start with that sound and place them in the tray.
4. Say the name of each object you found.
5. Choose a new letter card and repeat.

### Teacher Prep

- Fill the bin with 12 sound objects (3 each for m, s, t, a) and 6 distractors.
- Put a letter card (m, s, t, a) at the top of the table, with one tray beside it.
- Place the four letter cards in a stack to be turned over one at a time.

### Teacher Check

No adult supervision is needed during this center. Check the group's work at the end of the center.

## Center 3: Counting Trays

**Skill:** Count objects accurately to 10.

### Picture Direction Card (print and post)

[Picture: a tray with 10 blue cubes, a finger touching each cube in turn, and a number card showing 10 next to the tray.]

**Step 1:** Take a tray.
**Step 2:** Touch and count each object one time.
**Step 3:** Say the last number you counted.
**Step 4:** Check your answer with the number card.

### What Students Do

1. Choose a tray.
2. Touch each object and count out loud, one number for each object.
3. Say the total.
4. Turn over the number card to check.
5. Count a different tray if time allows.

### Teacher Prep

- Make 4 trays, each with 10 identical objects (beans, cubes, buttons, paper clips). Use a different object type on each tray.
- Make number cards 1 to 10, with a dot pattern and the numeral on each card.

### Teacher Check

Sit with the group for the first tray. Watch for students who skip objects or count the same object twice. Model touching and moving each object as you count.

## Center 4: Name Stamp

**Skill:** Recognize the first letter of your name and its sound.

### Picture Direction Card (print and post)

[Picture: a child's name card with the first letter circled in red, a stamp pad, and a stamp shape pressing onto paper.]

**Step 1:** Find your name card.
**Step 2:** Look at the first letter. Say its sound.
**Step 3:** Stamp or dot that letter on your paper.
**Step 4:** Stamp or dot a letter from another name, and say its sound.

### What Students Do

1. Find your name card.
2. Point to the first letter and say its sound.
3. Use a stamp or dot marker to make that letter on your paper.
4. If the first letter is m, s, t, or a, make it on the paper again. If not, find a letter from the class list that is m, s, t, or a and stamp it.

### Teacher Prep

- Write each student's first name on a card, with the first letter in a different color.
- Set out a stamp pad or dot markers, and several sheets of paper.
- Include a few blank strips for students to write their own letters.

### Teacher Check

Support students whose name starts with a letter outside m, s, t, a. Point to the matching letter on the target list and say its sound with them.

## Answer Key

Center answers are checked by the teacher during the first round of each center:

- **Sound Sort:** m: mop, mouse, moon, mitten. s: sun, sock, spoon, snake. t: top, tent, turtle, truck. a: apple, ant, ax, alligator.
- **Sound Hunt:** 3 objects each for m, s, t, a in the bin (teacher-selected), plus 6 distractors that do not start with those sounds.
- **Counting Trays:** each tray has exactly 10 objects. The number card should show 10.
- **Name Stamp:** the first letter of each child's name, stamped correctly. Accept any letter from the m, s, t, a list.
