## Activity 1: Worked Examples (25 minutes)

**Read aloud:** "We are going to look at three worked examples. Follow each step on your paper. Copy the steps as I go. Then you will try a similar problem on your own."

**Step-by-step method for any linear equation:**
1. Use the distributive property to remove parentheses, if there are any.
2. Combine like terms on each side of the equal sign.
3. Move variable terms to one side and constant terms to the other, using addition or subtraction.
4. Divide both sides by the number in front of the variable.
5. Check your answer by putting it back into the original equation.

**Example A: Solve 4x + 6 = 22**
- Subtract 6 from both sides: 4x = 16
- Divide both sides by 4: x = 4
- Check: 4(4) + 6 = 16 + 6 = 22. Correct.

**Example B: Solve 5x - 8 = 2x + 4**
- Subtract 2x from both sides: 3x - 8 = 4
- Add 8 to both sides: 3x = 12
- Divide both sides by 3: x = 4
- Check: 5(4) - 8 = 20 - 8 = 12, and 2(4) + 4 = 8 + 4 = 12. Correct.

**Example C: Solve 3(x - 2) = 2x + 1**
- Distribute: 3x - 6 = 2x + 1
- Subtract 2x from both sides: x - 6 = 1
- Add 6 to both sides: x = 7
- Check: 3(7 - 2) = 3(5) = 15, and 2(7) + 1 = 14 + 1 = 15. Correct.

**Read aloud:** "Now, with a partner, solve this one on your own paper: 2(x + 3) = 4x - 2. Take five minutes. Then compare with your partner."

*After five minutes, say:* "Check your answer: x = 4. Distribute to get 2x + 6 = 4x - 2, subtract 2x to get 6 = 2x - 2, add 2 to get 8 = 2x, and divide to get x = 4. Hands up if you got it."
