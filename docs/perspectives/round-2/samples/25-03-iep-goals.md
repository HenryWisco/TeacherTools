## Goal Option A: Oral Reading Fluency

**Annual goal:** By [date, one year from IEP start], when given a randomly selected grade 5 narrative passage of approximately 150 words at the student's assigned reading level, the student will read aloud at a rate of 100 words correct per minute (WCPM) with at least 95% accuracy on three consecutive probes.

- **Condition:** Randomly selected grade-level passage, read aloud, timed for one minute, with a teacher or assessor present.
- **Observable behavior:** Reads aloud words in connected text.
- **Criterion:** 100 WCPM with 95% accuracy on three consecutive probes.
- **Measurement:** Weekly one-minute oral reading probes scored for WCPM and accuracy, graphed by the teacher.

**Short-term objectives:**
1. By [date, about 12 weeks], the student will read a grade 4 passage aloud at 80 WCPM with 93% accuracy on three consecutive probes.
2. By [date, about 24 weeks], the student will read a grade 5 passage aloud at 90 WCPM with 95% accuracy on three consecutive probes.

## Goal Option B: Decoding Accuracy in Connected Text

**Annual goal:** By [date], when reading a grade 5 passage of about 150 words aloud, the student will read with at least 97% word accuracy, self-correcting errors in at least 80% of instances, on four of five consecutive weekly probes.

- **Condition:** Grade-level passage read aloud during structured reading time, with the student's own choice of passage from a teacher-approved set.
- **Observable behavior:** Decodes multisyllabic words and reads unfamiliar words accurately, and self-corrects errors.
- **Criterion:** 97% word accuracy and self-correction of at least 80% of errors, on four of five consecutive probes.
- **Measurement:** Running record or error analysis each week, recording error types (substitutions, omissions, self-corrections).

**Short-term objectives:**
1. By [date, about 12 weeks], the student will read multisyllabic words from a grade 4 word list with 90% accuracy, using a taught syllable-division strategy in at least 8 of 10 trials.
2. By [date, about 24 weeks], the student will read a grade 4 passage with 95% accuracy and self-correct 70% of errors.

## Goal Option C: Sustained Reading Engagement and Fluency Routines (ADHD-informed)

**Annual goal:** By [date], during a 15-minute independent or partner reading session on a grade 5 level text, the student will remain on task and read for the full session in at least 80% of observed intervals, on four of five consecutive weeks, while completing a self-monitoring checklist.

- **Condition:** 15-minute structured reading session with a timer, a self-monitoring checklist, and a visual progress tracker.
- **Observable behavior:** Stays engaged with reading material, returns to the text after distractions, and completes the self-check at the end of each interval.
- **Criterion:** On-task behavior in at least 80% of 2-minute observation intervals, on four of five consecutive weeks.
- **Measurement:** Interval recording by the teacher or paraprofessional; student self-rating compared with teacher data.

**Short-term objectives:**
1. By [date, about 12 weeks], the student will use a self-monitoring checklist during 10-minute reading sessions with 90% completion across five sessions.
2. By [date, about 24 weeks], the student will sustain on-task reading for 15 minutes in at least 80% of intervals, with no more than one teacher prompt per session.

## Suggested Accommodations and Supports

- Extended time on reading tasks and assessments.
- Text-to-speech or audiobook versions of grade-level texts, paired with print for fluency practice.
- Reduced-distraction workspace and chunked passages with visible section breaks.
- Preview of new vocabulary before reading.
- Timers, checklists, and visual progress charts to support attention and self-monitoring.
- Movement breaks or a brief task-switch between reading segments as needed.
- Access to high-interest, grade-appropriate texts at an accessible reading level.

Note: Accommodations should be chosen by the team based on documented need and should be used consistently across settings.

## Progress-Monitoring Ideas

- Weekly one-minute oral reading probes (WCPM and accuracy) using grade-level passages, graphed against the aim line.
- Biweekly error analysis to track decoding patterns and self-correction.
- Monthly interval-recording data for engagement goals.
- Quarterly review with the family, sharing graphed data and reporting progress in the same format used for all goals.
- Reassess the goals if data show the student is not making expected progress, and document the team's decisions.
