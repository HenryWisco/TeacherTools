# Lesson Plan: Puberty and Body Changes

**Name:** ______________________  **Date:** ____________

## Grade and Subject

Grade 5, PE & Health. Class length: 45 minutes.

## Learning Objectives

Students will be able to say the following at the end of the lesson:

1. I can name at least three physical changes that happen during puberty.
2. I can explain that puberty happens at different times for different people.
3. I can describe healthy habits that help me take care of my changing body.
4. I can use the vocabulary words correctly in a sentence.

## Standards

No standards were specified. Align to your district or state Health Education standards as needed.

## Materials

- Projector or whiteboard and markers
- Vocabulary cards (one set per pair, see Key Vocabulary)
- "Changes Sort" cards, printed and cut (one set per pair, see Guided Practice)
- Exit ticket slips (one per student)
- Chart paper for the class "Healthy Habits" list

## Key Vocabulary

- **Puberty:** The period of growth when a body changes from a child's body into an adult body.
- **Hormone:** A chemical made by the body that signals the body to grow or change.
- **Growth spurt:** A short time when a person grows quickly in height.
- **Hygiene:** Habits that keep the body clean and healthy, such as bathing and brushing teeth.
- **Sweat gland:** A small gland in the skin that makes sweat to cool the body.
- **Privacy:** Keeping your body and personal matters to yourself and to people you trust.

## Lesson Sequence

### Hook (5 minutes)

Write on the board: "Everyone's body changes over time. What is one way you have grown or changed since you were a baby?"

Ask students to think for 30 seconds, then share with a partner. Take 2 or 3 quick answers aloud. Say: "Today we are going to learn about one special time of change called puberty."

### Direct Instruction (10 minutes)

Teach the vocabulary words one at a time. Read the definition, then ask: "Can you think of an example?"

Explain the main ideas:

1. Puberty usually starts between about ages 8 and 13 for girls and about ages 9 and 14 for boys. Some people start earlier or later, and both are normal.
2. Hormones start the changes. Your brain sends signals, and your body responds.
3. Common changes include:
   - Growth spurts in height and weight
   - Body shape changes, such as wider hips in some people and broader shoulders in others
   - Sweat glands becoming more active, which can cause body odor
   - Oily skin and possible acne
   - Hair growth under the arms and on the body
   - For some people, breast development (girls) or voice change (boys)

Say: "Every body changes on its own schedule. Comparing yourself to others is not helpful, because everyone's timing is different."

Ask the class: "Why might it help to know what changes to expect?" Take two or three answers. Expected answers include: it prevents surprise, it helps you feel normal, and it helps you know what to do.

### Guided Practice: Changes Sort (10 minutes)

Pair students and give each pair a set of Changes Sort cards. Each card shows one body change or one healthy habit. Students sort the cards into two piles: "Puberty change" and "Healthy habit to support my body."

Sample cards and expected sorting:

- "Sweat glands become more active" = Puberty change
- "Growth spurt in height" = Puberty change
- "Oily skin and possible acne" = Puberty change
- "Showering daily and using deodorant" = Healthy habit
- "Eating breakfast with fruit and protein" = Healthy habit
- "Getting 9 to 11 hours of sleep" = Healthy habit (for Grade 5 students, 9 to 12 hours is the usual recommendation)
- "Voice gets deeper" = Puberty change
- "Drinking water during and after exercise" = Healthy habit

Circulate and ask each pair: "How do you know this card is a change, and not a habit?" Listen for reasoning, not just the correct pile.

Bring the class back together. Ask: "Which habit do you think helps the most with sweat and body odor?" Expected answer: daily bathing and clean clothes, with deodorant.

### Independent Practice (15 minutes)

Students complete this short writing task on their own.

Directions: Finish each sentence in your own words.

1. One change I learned about today is ______________________________________________.
2. One healthy habit that can help my changing body is ______________________________________________.
3. Puberty happens at different times for different people because ______________________________________________.
4. Use the word "hygiene" in a sentence: ______________________________________________.

Write answers on this sheet or in notebooks. Allow 10 minutes of writing, then 5 minutes for a quick share with a partner.

### Closure (5 minutes)

Create the class "Healthy Habits" chart on chart paper. Ask students to call out one habit, and write it on the chart. Aim for 5 to 8 items.

Close with this message: "Your body is changing in ways that are normal. Healthy habits help you feel your best. If you ever have questions, talk to a trusted adult such as a parent, school nurse, or doctor."

## Differentiation

**For students who need support:**
- Provide the vocabulary words with pictures or simple icons.
- Give a word bank for the independent practice sentences: sweat, sleep, shower, grow, normal, water.
- Allow students to answer orally or draw a picture instead of writing.
- Pre-sort two or three Changes Sort cards with the student as a model.

**For students ready for more:**
- Ask: "Why do you think the body needs more sleep during growth?" Expected answer: the body repairs and grows during sleep.
- Challenge students to write a short paragraph that explains puberty to a younger student, using at least three vocabulary words.
- Have students research one healthy habit and explain how it supports the body.

**Privacy note:** Keep discussion general and respectful. Do not ask students to share personal body details. Use a "question box" for anonymous questions, and answer them at the next lesson or refer them to the school nurse.

## Formative Assessment: Exit Ticket (use last 2 minutes or as a separate check)

Students answer on a slip of paper and hand it in as they leave.

1. Name one change that happens during puberty.
   Expected answer: any one of growth spurt, sweat glands becoming active, oily skin, body hair growth, voice change, or body shape change.

2. Name one healthy habit that supports your changing body.
   Expected answer: showering or bathing, using deodorant, eating healthy food, drinking water, or getting enough sleep.

3. True or false: Everyone starts puberty at exactly the same age.
   Expected answer: False.

**Teacher check:** Students who answer all three correctly have met the objectives. For students who miss question 3 or give a vague answer to question 1 or 2, plan a short small-group review at the next lesson.

## Answer Key

**Changes Sort:**
- Puberty change: growth spurt in height; sweat glands become more active; oily skin and possible acne; voice gets deeper
- Healthy habit: showering daily and using deodorant; eating breakfast with fruit and protein; getting 9 to 12 hours of sleep; drinking water during and after exercise

**Independent Practice:** Answers will vary. Accept any reasonable, accurate response that uses the correct vocabulary.

**Exit Ticket:**
1. Any listed puberty change is correct.
2. Any listed healthy habit is correct.
3. False.
