## Activity 1: Verb Ending Chart (5 to 20 minutes)

Put this chart on the board or on the screen. Read it aloud.

**Regular -ar verbs in the present tense**

Take the verb ending off the infinitive (for example, hablar, to speak) to get the stem: habl-. Then add the endings below.

| Subject | Ending | Example with hablar (to speak) | English meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo (I) | -o | hablo | I speak |
| tú (you, informal) | -as | hablas | you speak |
| él / ella / usted (he / she / you formal) | -a | habla | he / she / you speak |
| nosotros / nosotras (we) | -amos | hablamos | we speak |
| vosotros / vosotras (you all, informal, Spain) | -áis | habláis | you all speak |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes (they / you all) | -an | hablan | they / you all speak |

**Read this aloud to the class:** "Notice that the verb changes its ending for each subject. The subject is the person doing the action. Most of the time, the ending tells us who the subject is. Let's say the endings together. Yo, -o. Tú, -as. Él, ella, usted, -a. Nosotros, -amos. Ellos, ellas, ustedes, -an."

**Read this aloud next:** "Now I'll say a verb and you say the correct form with me. Yo, ... hablo. Tú, ... hablas. Él, ... habla. Nosotros, ... hablamos. Ellos, ... hablan."

**Second example.** Write this on the board and have students repeat after you: "Estudiar (to study). Yo estudio. Tú estudias. Ella estudia. Nosotros estudiamos. Ellos estudian."

**Pronunciation note:** Students may say "vosotros" and "áis" in class. Explain: "vosotros is used mostly in Spain. In most of Latin America, students will say ustedes instead."
