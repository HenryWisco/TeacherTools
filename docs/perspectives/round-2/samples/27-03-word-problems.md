## Part B: Exponential Growth

9. A bacteria culture starts with 500 cells and doubles every 3 hours. How many cells are present after 12 hours?

__________________________________________________________________________

10. Maria deposits $2,000 in an account that earns 4% interest compounded annually. Using the formula A = P(1 + r)^t, find the amount after 10 years. Round to the nearest cent.

__________________________________________________________________________

11. The population of a small town is 8,000 and grows at a rate of 2.5% per year. Write an exponential model for the population P after t years, then estimate the population after 15 years. Round to the nearest whole person.

## Part C: Combined and Multi-Step Problems

16. A rescue team is searching a forest from a helicopter. The helicopter is hovering 300 meters above a lake. The angle of depression from the helicopter to a boat is 24 degrees. Then the helicopter climbs and the angle of depression to the same boat becomes 18 degrees. Find how far the helicopter climbed, and the new horizontal distance from the helicopter's position to the boat, if the helicopter is directly above a point on the same line as the boat's first position. Hint: Draw two right triangles that share the horizontal distance d. Round to the nearest meter.

__________________________________________________________________________

17. A research team tracks a population of 1,200 fish in a lake. Over the first 4 years, the population grows by 8% per year. Then a disease reduces the population by 25% in year 5. In year 6, the population grows by 8% again. Find the population at the end of year 6. Round to the nearest whole fish. Then find the average yearly percent change from year 0 to year 6, using the formula (end/start)^(1/6) - 1, to the nearest tenth of a percent.

__________________________________________________________________________

18. A student is designing a triangular garden bed on a school grounds plan. Two sides of the triangle are 14 meters and 9 meters, and the angle between them is 62 degrees. Find the length of the third side to the nearest tenth of a meter. Then the student wants a fence along the perimeter. The student also learns the town's ten-year growth factor for property costs is 1.035 per year, and the fence costs $48 per meter today. Find the cost of the fence in 10 years, rounded to the nearest dollar.
(Note: use the Law of Cosines for the third side. Cost in 10 years = current cost x 1.035^10.)

## Answer Key

**1.** tan(θ) = 1.5 / 18 = 0.08333. θ = arctan(0.08333) ≈ 4.8 degrees.

**2.** h = 40 tan(35°) = 40 × 0.7002 ≈ 28.0 m.

**3.** h = 120 sin(50°) = 120 × 0.7660 ≈ 91.9, so about 92 m.

**4.** The angle of depression equals the angle of elevation from the spot, so the angle at the spot is 28°. h = 85 tan(28°) = 85 × 0.5317 ≈ 45.2 m.

**5.** d = 60 / tan(12°) = 60 / 0.2126 ≈ 282.2, so about 282 m.

**6.** sin(15°) = 2.4 / L, so L = 2.4 / 0.2588 ≈ 9.3 m.

**7.** Horizontal distance: the angle of depression equals the angle at the campsite, so d = 45 / tan(31°) = 45 / 0.6009 ≈ 74.9 m. Straight-line distance: L = 45 / sin(31°) = 45 / 0.5150 ≈ 87.4 m.

**8.** Let x be the horizontal distance from the second point to the base. Then h = x tan(58°) and h = (x + 30) tan(42°). So x tan(58°) = (x + 30) tan(42°). Since tan(58°) ≈ 1.6003 and tan(42°) ≈ 0.9004: 1.6003x = 0.9004x + 27.012, so 0.6999x = 27.012 and x ≈ 38.6 m. Then h = 38.6 × 1.6003 ≈ 61.8 m.

**9.** A = 500 × 2^(12/3) = 500 × 2^4 = 500 × 16 = 8,000 cells.

**10.** A = 2000 × 1.04^10 = 2000 × 1.480244 ≈ $2,960.49.

**11.** P = 8000(1.025)^t. After 15 years: 8000 × 1.025^15 = 8000 × 1.453790 ≈ 11,630 people.

**12.** Shares = 200 × 1.6^6 = 200 × 16.777216 ≈ 3,355 shares.

**13.** V = 150(0.88)^t. After 5 years: 150 × 0.88^5 = 150 × 0.527732 ≈ $79.16.

**14.** Ridership doubles every 1.5 years, so the growth factor per year is 2^(1/1.5) = 2^(0.6667) ≈ 1.5874. After 6 years: 300 × 2^(6/1.5) = 300 × 2^4 = 300 × 16 = 4,800 bikes.

**15.** A = 5000e^(0.06 × 8) = 5000e^0.48 ≈ 5000 × 1.616074 ≈ $8,080.37. Doubling time: t = ln 2 / 0.06 = 0.693 / 0.06 ≈ 11.6 years.

**16.** Let d be the horizontal distance to the boat. First position: 300 = d tan(24°), so d = 300 / 0.4452 ≈ 673.8 m. Second position: the angle of depression is 18°, so the new height is H = d tan(18°) = 673.8 × 0.3249 ≈ 218.9 m. The helicopter's height is therefore 218.9 m, which is lower than before, so it descended. Note: the helicopter moves down, not up. Net change in height: 218.9 − 300 = −81.1 m. The helicopter descended about 81 m and is about 674 m horizontally from the boat.

**17.** After year 4: 1200 × 1.08^4 = 1200 × 1.360489 ≈ 1,632.59. Year 5: 1632.59 × 0.75 ≈ 1,224.44. Year 6: 1224.44 × 1.08 ≈ 1,322.40, so about 1,322 fish. Average yearly change: (1322.40 / 1200)^(1/6) − 1 = (1.10200)^(1/6) − 1 ≈ 0.0165, so about 1.7% per year.

**18.** Third side: c^2 = 14^2 + 9^2 − 2(14)(9)cos(62°) = 196 + 81 − 252 × 0.4695 = 277 − 118.31 = 158.69. c ≈ 12.6 m. Perimeter = 14 + 9 + 12.6 = 35.6 m. Fence cost today = 35.6 × 48 ≈ $1,708.80. Cost in 10 years = 1708.80 × 1.035^10 = 1708.80 × 1.410599 ≈ $2,410 (rounded to the nearest dollar).
