# Beginning Letter Sounds: m, s, t, a

**Name:** ______________________  **Date:** ____________

Your teacher will read the directions aloud. Look at each picture. Say the name of the picture in your head. Then do what the box says: circle, color, or trace.

**Example:** Look at the picture of the sun. Say "sss-un." The first sound is /s/. The letter that makes /s/ is S. Trace the S.

## Part A: Circle the Picture

1. Circle the picture that starts with /m/. Choices: [apple] [mouse] [sock]
2. Circle the picture that starts with /s/. Choices: [sun] [cat] [hat]
3. Circle the picture that starts with /t/. Choices: [moon] [tiger] [apple]
4. Circle the picture that starts with /a/. Choices: [ant] [sock] [mop]

## Part B: Say It, Then Trace the Letter

Look at each picture. Trace the letter that makes the first sound.

5. [picture of a monkey]  Trace: M M M
6. [picture of a sock]  Trace: S S S
7. [picture of a tent]  Trace: T T T
8. [picture of an alligator]  Trace: A A A

## Part C: Find the Matching Sound

9. Look at the first picture in each row. Circle the picture in the row that starts with the same sound.
   - [milk] [moon] [car] [dog]
   - [sun] [bee] [sock] [ball]
   - [top] [fish] [lamp] [tree]

10. Color the pictures that start with /a/. Leave the others white.
    [apple] [bus] [ant] [cup] [astronaut] [pig]
