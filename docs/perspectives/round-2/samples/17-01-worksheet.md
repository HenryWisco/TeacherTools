**Directions:** Solve each problem. Show your work in the space provided. Write your final answer as a fraction or mixed number, and simplify when you can.

**Worked Example:** Three pizzas are shared equally among 4 friends. How much pizza does each friend get?
Think of it as 3 divided by 4. Each pizza is cut into 4 equal parts, so 3 pizzas make 3 groups of 1/4. Each friend gets 3/4 of a pizza.

Answer: 3/4 pizza each.
