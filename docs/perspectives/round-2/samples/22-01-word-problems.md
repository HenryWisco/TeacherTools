9. A store buys a shipment of 50 flashlights for $6.00 each. It sells all of them at a 50% markup, but 10 flashlights are damaged and must be discarded without any revenue. 10. A store owner takes out a loan of $4,000 at a simple interest rate of 6% per year for 2 years. She repays the loan at the end of 2 years with the interest included. After repayment, she sells a lawn mower that cost the store $250.00 at a markup of 40%, then charges 6% sales tax on the sale. How much did she sell the lawn mower for including tax, and how much interest did she pay on the loan in total?

## Answer Key

1. Selling price = $12.00 + (25% of $12.00) = $12.00 + $3.00 = $15.00. Alternatively, 12.00 x 1.25 = $15.00.

2. Markup = 40% of $8.00 = 0.40 x 8.00 = $3.20. Selling price = $8.00 + $3.20 = $11.20.

3. Sales tax = 6% of $15.00 = 0.06 x 15.00 = $0.90. Total cost = $15.00 + $0.90 = $15.90.

4. Discount = 20% of $80.00 = 0.20 x 80.00 = $16.00. Sale price = $80.00 - $16.00 = $64.00.

5. Cost before tax = 3 x $24.00 = $72.00. Tax = 7% of $72.00 = 0.07 x 72.00 = $5.04. Total bill = $72.00 + $5.04 = $77.04.

6. Interest = $2,000 x 0.05 x 1 = $100.00. Total repayment = $2,000 + $100 = $2,100.00.

7. Marked-up price = $60.00 + (30% of $60.00) = $60.00 + $18.00 = $78.00. Discount = 10% of $78.00 = $7.80. Sale price = $78.00 - $7.80 = $70.20.

8. Interest = $1,500 x 0.04 x 3 = $180.00. Total balance = $1,500 + $180 = $1,680.00.

9. Selling price per flashlight = $6.00 + 50% of $6.00 = $6.00 + $3.00 = $9.00. Flashlights that sell = 50 - 10 = 40. Revenue before tax = 40 x $9.00 = $360.00. Tax = 8% of $360.00 = 0.08 x 360.00 = $28.80. Total paid by customers = $360.00 + $28.80 = $388.80.

10. Interest on loan = $4,000 x 0.06 x 2 = $480.00. Lawn mower selling price before tax = $250.00 + 40% of $250.00 = $250.00 + $100.00 = $350.00. Sales tax = 6% of $350.00 = 0.06 x 350.00 = $21.00. Total price including tax = $350.00 + $21.00 = $371.00. Total interest paid on the loan = $480.00.
