### Passage

In our high-desert community, rain is rare. Some months pass with no rain at all. Because of this, families must plan carefully to get and keep the water they need.

Most families do not have water pipes running to their homes. Instead, they haul water from a **community well**. A community well is a deep hole in the ground that many people share. A pump brings water up to the surface. Families fill large containers and drive them home in trucks or on carts.

At home, water is stored in **cisterns** and barrels. A cistern is a large tank that holds water for later use. Some families catch rain from their roofs in gutters that pour into barrels. This rainwater is saved for watering gardens and animals.

Families save water in many ways. They wash dishes in a basin instead of running water. They reuse water from washing vegetables to water plants. They take short showers, and they fix leaks right away, because one slow drip can waste a lot of water over a month.

Water is precious here. Every drop counts, and every family works together to make sure it lasts.

## Answer Key

**Level 1**
1. From a community well (also acceptable: "They haul water from a community well.").
2. A cistern is a large tank that holds water for later use.
3. b. To save rainwater for gardens and animals.
4. Any one of: "They wash dishes in a basin instead of running water." / "They reuse water from washing vegetables to water plants." / "They take short showers..." / "they fix leaks right away..."
5. b. very valuable.
6. Sample: "Fixing leaks is important because one slow drip can waste a lot of water over a month."

**Level 2**
1. Sample: Rain is rare and some months have no rain, so families must plan ahead to get and keep enough water.
2. Sample: Families haul water from the community well by filling large containers and driving them home in trucks or on carts. (Accept any two steps: pump brings water up; fill containers; drive home.)
3. Reuse means using water again for another purpose. Context clue: the water from washing vegetables is used to water plants.
4. Sample: It means water is very valuable. Detail: "Every drop counts," or families take steps like fixing leaks and reusing water.
5. "Every drop counts, and every family works together to make sure it lasts." (Also acceptable: "Families save water in many ways.")
6. Sample: One drip wastes a lot of water over a month, so a small leak adds up to a large loss.

**Level 3**
1. Sample: A large cistern holds much more water in one place, so it can supply a family for longer during dry months. A family with only barrels would need more containers, may need to refill more often, and would rely on catching rain from gutters to fill each one.
2. Sample: The passage says rainwater is saved for gardens and animals, and in a dry climate water is precious. Using rain for plants and animals keeps the well and stored water for drinking and cooking.
3. 1 cup per hour x 24 hours = 24 cups per day (3 quarts, or 6 cups = 1.5 quarts ... precisely, 24 cups is 6 quarts). Over a month (about 30 days), 720 cups. The passage supports calling this "a lot" because it adds up over time. Accept reasoned answers that note the quantity depends on the family's needs.
4. Answers will vary. Sample: Reusing vegetable-wash water to water plants saves the most because it turns water that would be thrown away into a useful resource. Unmentioned strategy example: collecting shower water while it warms up.
5. "Haul" means carrying water from a distant source (the well) by vehicle or cart; "catch" means collecting rain where it falls. This shows the community relies on two sources: a shared well that must be transported, and rain collected at home.
6. Sample: "Every drop counts, and every family works together to make sure it lasts." The community well is a deep hole that many people share, so families depend on one another to use and protect it.
