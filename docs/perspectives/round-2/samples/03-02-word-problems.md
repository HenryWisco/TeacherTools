## Answer Key

### Version A

1. Unit rate: 12 minutes ÷ 3 laps = 4 minutes per lap. Mia takes 4 minutes per lap.

2. 9:12. Divide both by 3: 3:4. The ratio is 3:4.

3. Discount: 25% of $24 = 0.25 × 24 = $6. Sale price: $24 − $6 = $18. The sale price is $18.

4. Javier: $10.50 ÷ 5 = $2.10 per ball. Carla: $14.80 ÷ 8 = $1.85 per ball. Carla pays less per ball ($1.85 is less than $2.10).

5. Increase: $46 − $40 = $6. Percent increase: 6 ÷ 40 = 0.15 = 15%. The percent increase is 15%.

6. Sale price: 70% of $60 = 0.70 × 60 = $42. Coupon: 10% of $42 = 0.10 × 42 = $4.20. Final price: $42 − $4.20 = $37.80. The final price is $37.80.

7. Increase: 90 − 60 = 30. Percent increase: 30 ÷ 60 = 0.5 = 50%. The percent increase is 50.0%.

8. Original: 200 tickets in ratio 3:2 means 3 + 2 = 5 parts. Each part is 200 ÷ 5 = 40. Student tickets = 3 × 40 = 120. Adult tickets = 2 × 40 = 80. After 40 more adult tickets, adult tickets = 120. New ratio of student to adult = 120:120 = 1:1. The number of student tickets did not change; it stayed at 120. Only the adult count changed.

### Version B

1. Unit rate: 10 minutes ÷ 4 lengths = 2.5 minutes per length. Diego takes 2.5 minutes per length.

2. 15:20. Divide both by 5: 3:4. The ratio is 3:4.

3. Discount: 20% of $80 = 0.20 × 80 = $16. Sale price: $80 − $16 = $64. The sale price is $64.

4. Aisha: $18.00 ÷ 6 = $3.00 per puck. Ben: $28.00 ÷ 10 = $2.80 per puck. Ben pays less per puck ($2.80 is less than $3.00).

5. Increase: $60 − $50 = $10. Percent increase: 10 ÷ 50 = 0.20 = 20%. The percent increase is 20%.

6. Sale price: 80% of $90 = 0.80 × 90 = $72. Coupon: 10% of $72 = 0.10 × 72 = $7.20. Final price: $72 − $7.20 = $64.80. The final price is $64.80.

7. Increase: 100 − 75 = 25. Percent increase: 25 ÷ 75 = 1/3 ≈ 0.3333 = 33.3%. The percent increase is about 33.3%.

8. Original: 300 tickets in ratio 5:1 means 5 + 1 = 6 parts. Each part is 300 ÷ 6 = 50. Student tickets = 5 × 50 = 250. Adult tickets = 1 × 50 = 50. After 50 more adult tickets, adult tickets = 100. New ratio of student to adult = 250:100 = 5:2. The number of student tickets did not change; it stayed at 250. Only the adult count changed.
