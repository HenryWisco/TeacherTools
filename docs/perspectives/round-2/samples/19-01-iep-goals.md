# IEP Goal Ideas: Marcus T., Grade 4

**Name:** ______________________  **Date:** ____________

These are draft ideas for the IEP team to adapt, since goals must be individualized by the team based on the student's needs, input from the family, and current data.

## Present Level Summary

- **Reading fluency:** 52 words correct per minute (WCPM) on grade 3 text, 88% accuracy.
- **Written expression:** Writes 2-3 simple sentences using a graphic organizer; frequent run-on sentences.
- **Attention and movement:** Leaves seat about 6 times in a 30-minute lesson.

## Goal Option 1: Reading Fluency

**Annual goal:** Given a grade-level passage of 150 to 200 words read aloud, the student will read with at least 95% accuracy at a rate of 70 words correct per minute, as measured by three consecutive curriculum-based measurement probes.

- **Condition:** Given a grade-level passage (grade 4 text) and a teacher-timed one-minute reading probe.
- **Observable behavior:** Reads aloud orally, self-corrects errors within the sentence.
- **Criterion:** 70 WCPM with 95% accuracy.
- **Measurement:** Curriculum-based measurement (CBM) one-minute probes, given once weekly. Goal met when three consecutive probes meet criterion.

**Short-term objectives:**
1. By the end of the first quarter, the student will read a grade 3 passage at 60 WCPM with 92% accuracy on three consecutive weekly probes.
2. By the end of the second quarter, the student will read a grade 4 passage at 65 WCPM with 94% accuracy on three consecutive weekly probes.
3. By the end of the third quarter, the student will read a grade 4 passage at 70 WCPM with 95% accuracy on three consecutive weekly probes.

## Goal Option 2: Written Expression, Sentence Construction

**Annual goal:** Given a graphic organizer and a teacher-provided topic, the student will write a paragraph of at least six complete, grammatically correct sentences, with no more than one run-on sentence, on three of four consecutive writing samples scored with a rubric.

- **Condition:** Given a graphic organizer, a topic sentence starter, and a sentence-checklist card.
- **Observable behavior:** Writes complete sentences, each with a subject and predicate, and punctuates sentence endings correctly.
- **Criterion:** Six complete sentences with no more than one run-on, on three of four consecutive samples.
- **Measurement:** Monthly writing samples scored with the rubric below, plus a weekly count of run-on sentences.

**Short-term objectives:**
1. By the end of the first quarter, the student will write a paragraph of four complete sentences with no more than two run-ons, given a graphic organizer and sentence checklist, on three consecutive samples.
2. By the end of the second quarter, the student will write five complete sentences with no more than one run-on, using the checklist with teacher prompts on no more than half of sentences.
3. By the end of the third quarter, the student will write six complete sentences with no more than one run-on, using the checklist independently.

**Sentence Writing Rubric (for progress monitoring)**

| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 4 | Six or more complete sentences; no run-ons; all sentences end with correct punctuation |
| 3 | Five complete sentences; one run-on or punctuation error |
| 2 | Four complete sentences; two run-ons or punctuation errors |
| 1 | Fewer than four complete sentences or three or more run-ons |

## Goal Option 3: On-Task Behavior and Seat Leaving

**Annual goal:** During 30-minute whole-class and small-group instruction, the student will remain in his assigned seat for at least 80% of observed intervals, as measured by interval recording across five consecutive school days.

- **Condition:** During 30-minute instructional blocks, with a visual schedule, a movement break plan, and a self-monitoring card.
- **Observable behavior:** Remains seated in the assigned seat, or uses the agreed movement break and returns to seat within the agreed time.
- **Criterion:** Seated for at least 80% of 30-second intervals.
- **Measurement:** Teacher or paraprofessional interval recording (30-second intervals, 10-minute observation windows, three times weekly). Also track number of seat departures per lesson.

**Short-term objectives:**
1. By the end of the first quarter, the student will remain seated for at least 60% of intervals, with no more than four unplanned seat departures per 30-minute lesson.
2. By the end of the second quarter, the student will remain seated for at least 70% of intervals, using a self-monitoring card with teacher check-ins on no more than two occasions per lesson.
3. By the end of the third quarter, the student will remain seated for at least 80% of intervals and will use an agreed movement break, no more than twice per lesson, to meet sensory needs.

## Suggested Accommodations

- Preferential seating near the front and away from high-traffic areas.
- Scheduled movement breaks (for example, delivering materials or erasing the board) built into lessons.
- Visual schedule and timer for each activity segment.
- Chunked directions and reduced-length reading or writing tasks while building stamina.
- Access to a graphic organizer, sentence-starter bank, and sentence-checklist card for writing tasks.
- Read-aloud of directions and text as needed for independent work.
- Fidget or quiet seating tool, if the team agrees it does not distract peers.
- Positive behavior reinforcement tied to the self-monitoring card.

## Progress-Monitoring Ideas

- **Reading:** Weekly one-minute CBM probes on grade-level passages; graph WCPM and accuracy to show trend.
- **Writing:** Monthly sample scored with the rubric; weekly tally of run-on sentences on a class assignment.
- **Behavior:** Three 10-minute interval-recording observations per week; graph percentage of intervals seated; track seat departures per lesson.
- **Team review:** Review data every nine weeks and adjust objectives or supports as needed.
