# Honeybees Help Plants Grow

**Name:** ______________________  **Date:** ____________

Honeybees are very important helpers. Bees help plants make seeds and fruit.

A bee flies from flower to flower to get nectar. Nectar is a sweet drink that flowers make. As the bee moves, tiny dust grains called pollen stick to the hairs on her body. When she lands on the next flower, some of the pollen rubs off. This helps the plant make seeds and fruit.

Many foods we eat need bees to help them grow. Apples, blueberries, cucumbers, and almonds all need bees.

A healthy bee home in summer can have 20,000 to 60,000 bees. Almost all of them are female worker bees. There is one queen bee. Her main job is to lay eggs. On a very busy day, she can lay up to 1,500 eggs! The hive also has a few hundred male bees called drones.

Worker bees tell each other where to find flowers. They do a special dance called the waggle dance inside the dark hive. The way the bee turns shows which way to fly, compared to the sun. How long the bee wiggles shows how far away the flowers are.

Some bee families have been getting sick and dying in many places. Scientists do not think one thing is to blame. Tiny pests called mites, some bug sprays, sickness, and fewer wildflowers all make it hard for bees.

You can help bees! Plant flowers that grow naturally where you live. Do not spray bug killer on flowers when they are blooming. Put a shallow dish of water with small pebbles in it so bees have a place to land and drink.

## Glossary

**Pollen:** A tiny dust-like powder made by flowers. Bees carry it from flower to flower.

**Nectar:** A sweet liquid that flowers make. Bees drink it and use it to make honey.

**Pollinator:** An animal, such as a bee, that moves pollen from one flower to another.

**Hive:** The home where a group of bees lives.

**Queen:** The one female bee in a hive who lays the eggs.

**Drone:** A male bee in a hive.

**Waggle dance:** A dance a worker bee does to show other bees where to find flowers.

**Wildflower:** A flower that grows on its own in nature, without people planting it.
