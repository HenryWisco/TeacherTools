# Water Cycle: Key Words and Sentence Frames

**Name:** ______________________  **Date:** ____________

## Pati 1: Mo kle pou fè lespri ou (Key Words)

Gade mo yo nan tablo a. Li mo anglè a byen fò. Ekri mo kreyòl la ak definisyon an nan pwòp mo ou.

| Mo anglè (English word) | Mo kreyòl (Haitian Creole) | Definisyon an lang angle (Simple English definition) |
|---|---|---|
| water | dlo | Clear liquid we drink and use. Water is in rivers, lakes, and oceans. |
| evaporation | evaporasyon | When liquid water turns into gas (vapor) because of heat from the sun. |
| vapor | vapè | Water in the air as a gas. You cannot see it. |
| condensation | kondansasyon | When water vapor cools and turns back into tiny drops of liquid water. |
| cloud | nwaj | A group of tiny water drops or ice crystals floating in the sky. |
| precipitation | presipitasyon | Water that falls from clouds to the ground, like rain, snow, or hail. |
| collection | koleksyon | When water gathers in one place, like a lake, river, or ocean. |
| groundwater | dlo anba tè a | Water that soaks into the ground and is stored in the soil and rock. |

## Pati 2: Fè fraz ou yo (Sentence Frames)

Pratike di fraz sa yo an angle. Chak fraz gen tradiksyon kreyòl anba li pou ede ou konprann.

1. The sun heats the ____________ and the water changes into vapor.
   Solèy la chofe ____________ a epi dlo a chanje tounen vapè.

2. Water vapor rises into the air and ____________ into clouds.
   Vapè dlo a monte nan lè a epi li ____________ tounen nwaj.

3. When clouds get heavy, ____________ falls to the ground.
   Lè nwaj yo vin lou, ____________ tonbe sou tè a.

4. Water collects in ____________, lakes, and oceans.
   Dlo ranmase nan ____________, lak, ak lanmè.

5. Some water soaks into the ground and becomes ____________.
   Gen kèk dlo ki antre nan tè a epi li vin ____________.

6. The water cycle goes around and around, so water is ____________ again and again.
   Sik dlo a fè yon bagay ki toujou ap tounen, kidonk dlo a ____________ fwa sou fwa.

Ranpli espas vid yo ak mo ki nan Pati 1 la.

## Answer Key

1. ground (tè a / grenn tè) — Note: accept "ground" or "water" as close answers, since "heats the water" is also accurate in context.
2. condenses (kondanse) — Accept "condenses" as the key word.
3. precipitation (presipitasyon)
4. collection (koleksyon) — Accept "lakes," "rivers," or "collection" as the blank since the list is open.
5. groundwater (dlo anba tè a)
6. recycled (resikle) — Accept "reused" or "moving" as close answers.
