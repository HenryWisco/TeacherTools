## Answer Key

**Points:** Questions 1-4 are worth 2 points each. Questions 5-10 are worth 3 points each. Total: 26 points.

**Question 1 (2 points): B.** 35% of 80 = 0.35 × 80 = 28. Distractor A comes from moving the decimal the wrong way.

**Question 2 (2 points): B.** 25% of $60 = $15, so the sale price is $60 − $15 = $45. Wait, see note below.

**Question 3 (2 points): B.** The increase is $50. 50 ÷ 200 = 0.25, which is 25%.

**Question 4 (2 points): C.** 8% of $40 = $3.20, so the total is $40 + $3.20 = $43.20.

**Question 5 (3 points):** 40% of 30 = 0.40 × 30 = 12 students. Full credit requires converting 40% to 0.40 and multiplying.

**Question 6 (3 points):** Decrease = $50 − $35 = $15. Percent decrease = 15 ÷ 50 = 0.30, so 30%.

**Question 7 (3 points):** Tip = 0.15 × $64 = $9.60. Total = $64.00 + $9.60 = $73.60.

**Question 8 (3 points):** Discount = 20% of $30 = $6, so the discounted price is $24.00. Tax = 5% of $24 = $1.20. Final amount = $25.20.

**Question 9 (3 points):** No, she is not correct. Example: start with 100. A 20% increase gives 120. A 20% decrease of 120 is 24, so 120 − 24 = 96, not 100. Full credit requires a valid test number and a clear explanation that the decrease is calculated from the new, larger value.

**Question 10 (3 points):** Offer X: 30% off $100 = $70. Offer Y: 10% off gives $90; then 20% off $90 = $18, so $72. Offer X ($70) is the lower price. Full credit requires both totals, a comparison, and a choice with reasoning.
