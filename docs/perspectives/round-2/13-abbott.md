# Mr. Abbott, 6th grade science teacher and union building representative
**Verdict:** Would use, with conditions. I would not recommend it to my building until the money story is told the same way on every page and the answer keys stop contradicting themselves.

**In one sentence:** It is honestly free and does not ask for my name, which I respect, but two of the three answer keys I generated had mistakes a tired teacher would miss, and the site says three different things about how it makes money off teachers.

## What I did

Pages read in full: home (/), tools list (/tools), the Lab or Hands-On Activity tool page (/tools/lab-activity), About (/about), Support us (/support), Privacy (/privacy), Terms (/terms), Library (/library), the grades 6-8 science filter of the library, My documents (/my), and the library resource "Density Column Lab: Layering Household Liquids" (grade 7). I also skimmed the library resource "Newton's Three Laws of Motion: Stations Lesson Plan" (grade 8) and checked the Quiz and Family Email tool pages for ads only.

Documents generated (3):

1. `13-01-quiz.md` - Quiz tool. 10 mixed questions on density, grade 6 science, at least 4 calculations, no calculators.
2. `13-02-lab-activity.md` - Lab tool. Heat transfer / which cup keeps water warm, grade 6, NGSS MS-PS3-3. I told it: 30 students in groups of 4, one 45-minute period, no hot plates or flame, only 8 thermometers, one student with a latex allergy.
3. `13-03-anything.md` - The free-form box. Pond food chains and energy pyramids, exactly 8 questions (3 + 3 + 2), 10 percent rule from 20,000 kcal, answer key separate, no "worksheet" in the title, Versions A and B.

All three are saved in `C:\Users\henry\Desktop\TeacherTools\docs\perspectives\round-2\samples\`.

## Problems found

### Real mistakes in the documents

**1. Quiz answer key gives the wrong answer, then corrects itself in the same line.** (major)
Where: `13-01-quiz.md`, answer key, question 8.
What I saw: "8. A. Object X has a mass of 30 g and Object Y has a mass of 60 g with equal volumes, so Object X has the lower density. Correction note: Object Y is denser, so the correct answer is B."
The right answer is B. A teacher marking from the first letter of each line marks every correct student wrong. This looks like the AI thinking out loud and it should never reach the page.
Fix: the key must give one answer per question. Have the site check every key line for a second answer letter or words like "correction" before showing it, and redo the document if it finds one.

**2. Quiz says it is out of 30 points, but the points add up to 21.** (major)
Where: `13-01-quiz.md`, the Score line at the top ("______ / 30") and "Total: 30 points" at the bottom.
What I saw: the points in the key are 1 + 1 + 1 + 3 + 3 + 4 + 3 + 1 + 1 + 3 = 21.
A student who gets everything right scores 21 out of 30, which is 70 percent. That goes in a gradebook and home to a parent.
Fix: the total must be added up from the per-question points, not written separately.

**3. Lab does not fit the 45-minute period I asked for.** (major)
Where: `13-02-lab-activity.md`, Procedure steps 5 and 6.
What I saw: step 5 is "Every 2 minutes for 20 minutes, read and record the temperature," then step 6 is "Repeat steps 2 to 5 with the paper cup, metal cup, and plastic control cup." Four cups at 20 minutes each is 80 minutes before setup, cleanup or the graph. The same step then says to rotate the one thermometer among the cups "where possible" and that the teacher "may choose to shorten the timing to 16 minutes." So the procedure describes two different labs and leaves me to work out which. The document never states the total time or gives a minute-by-minute plan.
Fix: write one procedure that fits the stated period (all four cups filled at once, one thermometer moved cup to cup on a fixed schedule, about 15 minutes of readings) and print a timing line at the top.

**4. Lab is not a fair test: only one cup gets a lid.** (major for a science teacher)
Where: `13-02-lab-activity.md`, Materials and Procedure step 3.
What I saw: materials list "paper (paper cup with a lid)" while the other three cups have none, and step 3 says "Put the lid on the cup if it has one." Most heat leaves an open cup from the top, so the lidded cup wins regardless of material. The whole point of 6th grade lab work is controlling variables. The "plastic control" cup is also not a control; it is just a fourth material, and the key's explanation ("shows how the water cools without a special material") is not right.
Fix: all cups lidded or none; call the plastic cup a fourth material or drop it.

**5. Lab refers to things that are not there.** (minor)
Where: `13-02-lab-activity.md`, step 8 and step 2.
What I saw: "Graph your group's results on the grid provided" - there is no grid. Step 2 says to record the room-temperature reading "as the starting temperature," but the data table's starting temperature is the hot water at Time 0. Also 300 mL x 4 cups x 8 groups is nearly 10 litres of hot tap water, which nobody warned me about.
Fix: include a blank grid or say "on graph paper"; remove the confusing step 2 wording; reduce to about 150 mL per cup.

**6. Version B answer key contradicts the food web it gave the students.** (major)
Where: `13-03-anything.md`, Answer Key, Version B, question 8.
What I saw: "If the water snails were removed, tadpoles would lose a food source, so they might increase at first because fewer snails compete with them for algae." In the food web on the same page, tadpoles eat algae and pondweed, not snails. The sentence also says two opposite things. The next sentence about bass ("which depend on snails less directly") is muddled too.
Fix: rewrite as: tadpoles have less competition for algae and may increase; dragonfly nymphs and small fish lose one food source; bass are affected in turn.

**7. Versions A and B have identical calculation questions.** (minor)
Where: `13-03-anything.md`, questions 4 to 6 in both versions.
What I saw: both versions say "Use these energy values for Version A / Version B" and then both use 20,000 kcal with answers 2,000, 200 and 20. I did ask for 20,000, so this is partly on me, but the point of a Version B is that neighbours cannot copy numbers, and the sheet announces different values that are not different.
Fix: when two versions are asked for, vary the numbers in B (or say plainly that the numbers are the same because the teacher fixed them).

**8. Standard is misquoted.** (minor)
Where: `13-02-lab-activity.md`, "Standard alignment."
What I saw: "design, test, and refine a device that minimizes or maximizes thermal energy transfer." The published wording of MS-PS3-3 is "design, construct, and test a device that either minimizes or maximizes thermal energy transfer." Also the lab is a comparison of cups, not a design task; only question 7 touches design.
Fix: do not print standard wording in quotes-like form unless it is exact; say "supports" rather than implying full alignment.

### Contradictions on the site about money

**9. The site tells three different stories about how it is paid for.** (major)
Where: home page "Free means free" box, /support, /about, and the newsletter sign-up in the footer of every page.
What I saw:
- Home: "The site is paid for by a few quiet ads and optional supporters."
- Support: "a few quiet ads cover that."
- About: "A small number of ads, links to classroom supplies that may earn a commission, an occasional sponsor in the weekly email, and optional supporters."
- Newsletter box on every page: "One email a week. The best new free resources, nothing else."

"Nothing else" and "an occasional sponsor in the weekly email" cannot both be true. And the Amazon commission links are real - I saw them on library pages - but the home and support pages leave them out. I do not object to a one-person free site earning its keep. I object to finding the full list only on the About page.
Fix: use the About page's full list everywhere money is mentioned, and change the newsletter line to say sponsors may appear, or drop sponsors.

**10. Affiliate shopping links are generic, not tied to the lesson.** (minor, but it is advertising aimed at teachers)
Where: /library/density-column-liquid-layers-lab-grade-7 and /library/newtons-three-laws-of-motion-lesson-plan-grade-8, the "Handy for this lesson" box.
What I saw: the same three Amazon links on both - "Safety goggles, class set," "Graduated cylinders & beakers," "Magnifying glasses." Magnifying glasses have nothing to do with a density column, and none of the three is in the Newton's laws materials list. Calling it "Handy for this lesson" is not accurate, and it nudges teachers to spend their own money, which the Support page itself says we already do too much of.
Fix: only show items that are actually in that resource's materials list, retitle the box honestly ("Supplies - affiliate links"), and put the commission sentence above the links rather than below.

### Policy and ownership gaps

**11. The terms do not say who owns what, or what the site may do with work teachers share.** (major for me as a rep)
Where: /terms, the "Your documents are yours to use" and "Sharing" lines.
What I saw: "Your documents are yours to use" is not the same as "you own them and we claim nothing." For sharing: "you allow other teachers to use and adapt it freely." It says nothing about what the site itself may do. A shared teacher document becomes a library page carrying an ad slot and Amazon commission links, so the owner earns from teachers' unpaid contributions and the terms are silent on that. There is no credit to the teacher, no promise that shared work will not be sold or bundled later, and nothing on whether a district could claim work made here.
Fix: add plain sentences: the site claims no ownership of anything you generate; shared documents stay free to all and will never be sold or put behind a paywall; state openly that library pages carry ads; say removal requests are honoured within a stated number of days.

**12. Nobody is named, and there is no way to contact anyone.** (major)
Where: /privacy "Contact" section, /about, /support.
What I saw: "Questions or removal requests: the site owner." No name, no email address, no form. Support says "Tell us what's missing" with no way to do it. Terms says "Ask the site owner to remove a shared document at any time" - how? About never says the site is run by one person or who they are. My first question about any tool pushed at teachers is who is behind it and who profits; this site does not answer it.
Fix: put a real contact email on Privacy, Terms, About and Support, and say on About that it is one person, with a name or at least a business name.

**13. Notes to the owner are showing on public pages.** (minor now, blocker if still there at launch)
Where: bottom of /privacy and /terms.
What I saw: "Site owner: review this page with a qualified adviser before launch, and add your business name and address." and "Site owner: review this page with a qualified adviser before launch."
This tells me the legal pages have not been reviewed yet.
Fix: get the review done and remove the notes before launch.

**14. Privacy page is silent on what the AI company does with what I type.** (minor)
Where: /privacy, "What we collect."
What I saw: what I type "is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic)... and is not stored by us." Nothing about whether the provider keeps it, for how long, or uses it for training. Teachers will paste their own lesson notes and school templates into the "Your own text" box.
Fix: one sentence on the provider's retention and training position, with a link.

**15. Library pages do not say the resource was written by AI or who reviewed it.** (minor)
Where: /library/density-column-liquid-layers-lab-grade-7 (read in full).
What I saw: the page shows type, grade and subject only. The library header says "Ready-made and reviewed," but no reviewer, date or "AI-written" label appears on the resource. Given the key errors I found in freshly generated documents, I want to know what "reviewed" means.
Fix: a line on each library page: written by AI or shared by a teacher, checked by whom and when.

### Opinions

**16. "Never will" is a promise one person cannot guarantee.** (minor, opinion)
Where: /support - "Supply Closet has no paywall and never will."
I have heard "free forever" from education companies before. Fix: say what happens to the site and the library if the owner stops or sells.

**17. The pitch is all about speed, with nothing about the teacher's judgment or time.** (minor, opinion)
Where: home page headline - "Whatever your class needs tomorrow, made tonight in seconds."
"Made tonight" takes for granted that I am working at night unpaid. And "in seconds" is exactly the line an administrator quotes when arguing we need less prep time. With the errors above, checking each document properly took me longer than generating it. Fix: say somewhere prominent that these are drafts and reviewing them is real professional work.

## What worked

- No account, no email required, and it really did not ask me for anything. Three documents in under 15 seconds each.
- The density quiz followed my instructions: exactly 10 questions, mixed types, 5 calculation questions with friendly no-calculator numbers, units required, and it used the triple beam balance and graduated cylinder in a displacement question. The calculation answers (4, 9, 8.4 and 1.25) are all correct.
- The lab took my safety limits seriously: hot tap water only, a 50 °C cap, no hot plate or flame, and a clear latex note ("Do not substitute rubber bands, latex gloves, or balloons. If using gloves, use nitrile or vinyl").
- The free-form box obeyed every counting instruction: exactly 8 questions split 3 / 3 / 2, started from 20,000 kcal, no "worksheet" in the title, two versions, key at the end. The 10 percent arithmetic is right in both versions.
- The library density lab (grade 7) is good. I worked the key: 47.5 g / 50 mL = 0.95 g/mL sits between oil and water, the 1.20 g/cm^3 stopper rests on the corn syrup, the sample densities are realistic, and the safety section on rubbing alcohol is what I would write myself.
- The About page is honest about AI: "AI can still make mistakes, so please read anything before you hand it to students."
- The ad rules are specific and I could confirm part of them: the Family Email tool page showed no ad slot while the Quiz tool page did, matching the promise of no ads beside family messages, report comments, IEP goals or differentiation. Ads are stated to be non-personalized and never on printed pages.
- Supporters get only "no ads." Nothing is held back from teachers who do not pay.

## What I could not check

- How anything looks: layout, colours, how intrusive the ads are, phone view.
- What Print, Word, Copy for Google Docs, Edit, Share and the Revise buttons do, and whether the answer key really prints on its own sheet (in the text I saw it was simply the last section).
- Whether ads and affiliate links really stay off printed and downloaded copies.
- What real ads will appear; I only saw placeholders reading "Ad space."
- The My documents page (it only showed "Loading...").
- The newsletter and the share-to-library flow, which I was told not to use.
- The library has only two resources for grades 6-8 science and none for grade 6, so I could not judge a library resource at my own grade.

## What I would say to a colleague about this site

It is free, it does not want your name, and it is fast. Use it for a first draft and nothing more. Work every answer key yourself before you copy it: in three documents I got one key that gave the wrong letter and then corrected itself mid-sentence, one point total that was off by nine, a lab that would take two periods instead of one, and a key that contradicted its own food web. As your building rep I would add: do not let anyone tell you this replaces prep time, because checking it properly is prep time. And before you share anything you made to their library, know that the page it lands on carries ads and shopping links, the terms do not say what the owner can do with it, and right now there is no name or email address to write to if you want it taken down.
