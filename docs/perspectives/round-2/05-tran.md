# Ms. Tran, kindergarten teacher

**Verdict:** Undecided. Leaning toward "would not use" for anything I put in front of children, but I might use it to plan.

**In one sentence:** The planning ideas and the one ready-made counting sheet in the library are sound, but everything the site generates for my non-readers has written labels like "[mouse]" or "[Picture: 7 hats]" where the pictures should be, so I would have to draw or find every picture myself, which is most of the work.

## What I did

- Pages visited: home page (/), Tools (/tools), Library (/library), the K-2 filter (/library?band=k-2), the kindergarten counting worksheet (/library/counting-comparing-numbers-to-10-worksheet-kindergarten), the grade 1 CVC phonics lesson (/library/short-vowel-cvc-words-lesson-plan-grade-1), and About (/about). Looked at the form fields for Worksheet and Ask for Anything.
- Documents generated (3):
  1. `05-01-worksheet.md`: Worksheet, Kindergarten, ELA, "Beginning letter sounds: m, s, t, a", 10 questions, mixed. In "Anything else" I wrote that my students cannot read and need picture-based items and read-aloud directions.
  2. `05-02-worksheet.md`: Worksheet, Kindergarten, Math, "Counting objects to 10 and matching to the numeral", 8 questions, again saying it is for non-readers and should use pictures of objects.
  3. `05-03-anything.md`: Ask for Anything, Kindergarten: a one-week centers plan, 4 centers, 15 minutes each, groups of 5 or 6, letters m, s, t, a and counting to 10, with a picture direction card per center and a rotation chart.

## Problems found

1. **No pictures: generated sheets for non-readers are unusable as printed.** (Blocker)
   - Where: all three generated documents.
   - What I saw: letter sounds sheet: "Circle the picture that starts with /m/. Choices: [apple] [mouse] [sock]" and "[picture of a monkey] Trace: M M M". Counting sheet: "[Picture: 4 balls]", followed by a note telling me to draw "the stated number of identical, simple objects in one box". Centers plan: each "Picture Direction Card" is a sentence like "[Picture: a child's hand reaching into a bin, pulling out a toy turtle...]" with the steps written out as words underneath.
   - Why it matters: a 5-year-old cannot read "[mouse]". For the counting sheet I would have to draw 51 objects by hand (2+4+5+7+8+6+9+10). The "direction cards" are written instructions, which is exactly what my class cannot follow. The site did not do the one thing I asked for.
   - Fix I'd want: real images (clip art or simple icons) for picture items, or at least simple symbols like the dots (●) the library sheet uses for counting. If the site can't do pictures, it should say so on the form when Kindergarten is picked, instead of printing placeholders that look finished.

2. **Answer key for question 9 on the letter sounds sheet is garbled and contradicts itself.** (Major)
   - Where: `05-01-worksheet.md`, Answer Key, item 9.
   - What I saw: "Circle **tree** in row 3 (top)? No: circle **top** is the first picture, so circle the matching picture **tree** only if it starts with /t/; the correct choice in row 3 is **top**, but top is the model, so circle **tree**..." It ends on "tree", which is right, but it reads like the AI arguing with itself.
   - Also: "tree" starts with the blend /tr/, a poor pick for children just learning a single /t/ sound. "Top" is the model, so there is no other /t/ choice in that row.
   - Fix: check answer keys for this kind of thinking-out-loud before showing them. Avoid blends (tree, truck) when the target is one beginning sound.

3. **Rotation chart says two different things.** (Major)
   - Where: `05-03-anything.md`, "Rotation Schedule".
   - What I saw: "Each group visits every center once during the week." But the chart has every group going through all four centers in one 60-minute block ("Minutes 0 to 15" ... "Minutes 45 to 60"), which works out to every center every day. It also says "Groups rotate clockwise", but the rows don't follow one fixed order (Group A: Sort, Hunt, Trays, Stamp; Group B: Trays, Sort, Stamp, Hunt), so children can't just "move to the next table". The chart itself does work: no two groups are at the same center at the same time.
   - Also: 60 minutes of centers a day is long for kindergarten. I asked for 15-minute centers, not an hour.
   - Fix: pick one schedule (for example one or two centers a day, or a true clockwise rotation) and make the words match the chart.

4. **The counting center always has the answer 10.** (Major)
   - Where: `05-03-anything.md`, Center 3 "Counting Trays" and the Answer Key.
   - What I saw: "Put 10 identical small objects in each tray" and the answer key "each tray has exactly 10 objects. The number card should show 10." It also tells me to make number cards 1 to 10, which then go unused.
   - Why it matters: after one tray the children know the answer is 10 and stop counting. That isn't practice counting to 10.
   - Fix: give the trays different amounts (for example 3, 6, 8, 10) and match each to a number card.

5. **The Name Stamp center contradicts itself, and the "no supervision" advice is unsafe.** (Minor to major)
   - Where: `05-03-anything.md`, Center 4 and Center 2.
   - What I saw: on the Name Stamp card, Step 4 says "Stamp or dot a letter from another name". The "What Students Do" list says "If the first letter is m, s, t, or a, make it on the paper again. If not, find a letter from the class list that is m, s, t, or a." Sound Hunt says "No adult supervision is needed during this center", and the plan uses bins of small objects and "dried beans" with 5-year-olds.
   - Fix: make the card and the steps say the same thing. Drop "no adult supervision" for kindergarten and add a choking-hazard note about small objects.

6. **The plan is laid out like a student worksheet.** (Minor)
   - Where: `05-03-anything.md`, top.
   - What I saw: "**Name:** ______ **Date:** ______" at the top of a teacher planning document.
   - Fix: leave the name line off plans meant for the teacher.

7. **The letter sounds sheet tells children to say words "in your head", and uses capital letters only.** (Minor)
   - Where: `05-01-worksheet.md`, directions and Part B.
   - What I saw: "Say the name of the picture in your head." Kindergartners need to say it out loud. The tracing is "M M M", "S S S", capitals only, with no lowercase m, s, t, a, even though the centers plan uses lowercase letter cards.
   - Fix: say "out loud", and offer both capital and lowercase tracing.

8. **Very little kindergarten content in the library, and the shopping links don't fit.** (Minor)
   - Where: /library?band=k-2 and the kindergarten counting worksheet page.
   - What I saw: K-2 has 3 resources, and only one is kindergarten. Under the kindergarten counting sheet, "Handy for this lesson" links to "Fraction tiles & circles" and "Base ten blocks", and "More like this" lists Grade 4, 8 and 9 math.
   - Fix: add kindergarten resources (letter sounds, counting, centers), and match the supply links and "More like this" to the grade.

## What worked

- The library counting worksheet for kindergarten is good. It uses dots (●) children can actually count, says "Listen as your teacher reads each part", and has sensible teacher notes (accept reversed numerals, touch each dot). I counted its 15 items and checked every answer. All correct.
- Every answer on my generated counting sheet was correct, and the number choices were sensible (the right one was never always in the same spot).
- The centers plan had useful ideas I would steal for my own planning: the sound sort word lists (mop, mouse, moon, mitten / sun, sock, spoon, snake / apple, ant, ax, alligator are all correct beginning sounds), group symbols (sun, star, ladybug, fish) so non-readers can find their row, and a prep list.
- The site is fast (3 to 15 seconds), free, and doesn't need an account. The About page plainly says AI can make mistakes and to read everything first.

## What I could not check

- Whether the "Trace" letters print as dotted letters, or just as plain letters.
- How anything looks printed, on a phone, or in Word or Google Docs.
- Whether the "Large print" or "Easier" buttons help young children.
- Whether pictures appear once a document is printed. I only saw text, and the text says "[picture of ...]".

## What I would say to a colleague about this site

"It's fine for getting ideas for a centers week or checking which words start with which sound, but don't expect anything you can hand to the kids. Where the pictures should be, it prints words like '[mouse]', so you'll be drawing or cutting out clip art for every item anyway. The one ready-made kindergarten counting sheet in the library is good, though. Read the answer key before you trust it: mine had a line where the computer was arguing with itself."
