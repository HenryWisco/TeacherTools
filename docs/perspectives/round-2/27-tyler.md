# Tyler, secondary math student teacher
**Verdict:** Would use with conditions. I would not hand any of these out without redoing the answer key.
**In one sentence:** The worksheet was clean, but the quiz and the word-problem set both had wrong answer-key entries, and nothing in the documents told a trusting user to check them.

## What I did
- Pages visited: home (/), /tools, /library?subject=math, and the worksheet and quiz forms. Also the word-problems form.
- Documents generated (3):
  1. 27-01-worksheet.md: Solving quadratics by factoring and the quadratic formula, grade 10, 10 questions.
  2. 27-02-quiz.md: Systems of linear equations and slope-intercept form, grade 9, 10 questions.
  3. 27-03-word-problems.md: Right triangle trig and exponential growth, grade 10. I did not set a count.
- I worked every answer by hand and recomputed the numbers.

## Problems found
1. **Quiz Q9 answer key contradicts itself.** The key first says "x = 4, y = 5/3", then in the same entry says "Correct solution: x = 2, y = 3". The right answer is (2, 3). A teacher who skims or copies the key would mark students wrong, or print the garbled line. Major. Fix: check the key and regenerate any entry where the key changes its own answer mid-line.
2. **Quiz Q4 key is wrong.** It says "C and D are the same line (infinitely many solutions)". D is y = 3x and y = 3x + 6, which are parallel with no solution. Only C is the same line. The final answer (B) is right, but a student who asks about D gets a false explanation. Major.
3. **Quiz Q10 key is wrong in context.** It says "for fewer months, such as none, the first is cheaper". At 0 months gym 1 costs $25 and gym 2 costs $0, so gym 1 is more expensive before month 1. Minor to major. The math (m = 1, both $40) is right.
4. **Word problems Q11 key is wrong.** It uses 1.025^15 = 1.453790 and gets 11,630. The true value is about 1.4483, so the population is about 11,587. Major. Students who calculate correctly will be marked wrong.
5. **Word problems Q17 key is wrong.** The average yearly change is (1.102)^(1/6) - 1, about 1.6%. The key says "about 1.7%" (it wrote 0.0165). Major.
6. **Word problems Q16 is impossible as written, and the key admits it.** The problem says the helicopter "climbs" and the angle of depression drops from 24 to 18 degrees over the same horizontal distance. That means it went down. The key says "Note: the helicopter moves down, not up." Blocker for that question. It cannot be handed out. Fix: have the site check the answer key against the question wording and drop or rewrite problems where they disagree.
7. **Word problems Q4 is ambiguous.** "The drone is 85 meters from a spot on the ground" with a 28 degree angle of depression. The key treats 85 as horizontal (85 tan 28 = 45.2 m). If 85 is the straight-line distance, the answer is about 39.9 m. Students will argue. Minor to major.
8. **Strange or nonsense problem text.** Q13 reads "cold brew concentrate is cooling from 90 degrees Celsius. Newton's law of cooling is not needed here; instead, a ceramic mug's internal storage value loses 12%...". This is leftover AI thinking text and confusing. Q14 mixes bikes and ridership ("so the number of active bikes is needed for planning"). Q18's fence cost grows with "property costs" and is not realistic. Q16 has a garbled sentence: "directly above a point on the same line as the boat's first position". Major for a teacher who would have to clean these up.
9. **Count not controlled for word problems.** The form offers 5, 8, 10 or 12 problems. I did not pick one and got 18, which is a lot for one sheet. Minor. Fix: default to one of the offered counts.
10. **Nothing in the documents prompts the teacher to check.** There is no "check the answer key before use" line on any of the three documents. The only reminder is small footer text on the site: "Always review materials before using them with your class." Someone who trusts AI as much as I do would never read it. Major for this user group. Fix: put a short "AI-made, please check every answer before use" line on the document itself, in the print header or the answer key page.
11. **Quiz Q1 is trivial.** "What is the slope-intercept form?" is recall, in a grade 9 quiz worth 2 points. Minor, an opinion about quality.

Count check: worksheet had 10 of 10, quiz had 10 of 10 with 30 points adding up correctly. Worksheet answer key: I checked all 10 and found no errors.

## What worked
- The worksheet was fully correct, with a worked example and a check on the harder factoring item.
- Layout is print-ready with a name and date line and the answer key on its own section.
- The tools were fast (8 to 17 seconds) and the form is simple.
- The library has real grade 9 math items such as the factoring worksheet. I did not read these in full.

## What I could not check
- Colours, layout, phone view, and what the Print, Word and Google Docs buttons do.
- Whether library resources are actually reviewed, as the library page says. I only read the listing.
- The About, Privacy and Terms pages. I did not open them.

## What I would say to a colleague about this site
It is a fast way to get a first draft, and the worksheet was good. But three out of three documents needed me to check, and two of them had real mistakes in the answer key. The wrong answers are exactly the kind a student teacher might copy straight out. Check every answer yourself, and do not trust the key just because it shows its work. The site should warn you on the page itself.
