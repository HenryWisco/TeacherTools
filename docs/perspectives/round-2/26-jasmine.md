# Jasmine, elementary education student teacher in her final placement

**Verdict:** Would use it, with conditions. I would only trust it for my observation lesson after the cut-off problem is fixed and I had checked every line myself.

**In one sentence:** The template option worked better than I expected: it really used my university's headings, and the math was right. But my first lesson plan stopped partway through, with the required reflection section missing, and both plans had small inconsistencies that my supervisor would notice.

## What I did

- Pages visited: home page (/), /tools, /tools/lesson-plan, the lesson plan forms for lesson-plan and exit-ticket, /library?type=lesson-plan, the library's Grade 1 "Short Vowel CVC Words Lesson Plan", /about, /privacy, /terms, /my.
- I made up a realistic 12-section university "Teacher Candidate Formal Lesson Plan (Observation Template)" and pasted it into the "Your own text or template" box. Its sections were Candidate Information, Central Focus, Standards (full text), ABCD Learning Objective, Academic Language (a–d), Prior Knowledge and Assets, Materials, Instructional Procedures with minutes, Differentiation (IEP/504, ELL, Advanced), Assessment (a–c), Classroom Management and Post-Lesson Reflection. The template said: "Use ALL headings below, in this order, with these exact names. Do not add or skip sections."
- Documents generated:
  1. `samples/26-01-lesson-plan.md`: Grade 3 math, comparing fractions with the same numerator or denominator, 45 minutes, CCSS 3.NF.A.3.D, my template pasted in. **It was cut off partway through.**
  2. `samples/26-02-exit-ticket.md`: a matching exit ticket for the same lesson.
  3. `samples/26-03-lesson-plan.md`: a second try at the lesson plan, the same inputs plus "Keep it concise so every template section fits, including the reflection."

## Problems found

1. **The first lesson plan was cut off and lost required sections.** (26-01-lesson-plan, end of document)
   It stopped mid-word in Classroom Management: "Assign one student per table as materials man". After that came only "This document reached the length limit. Ask for a shorter version…". Section 12, Post-Lesson Reflection, which my template requires, never appeared, and the practice problems had no answer key. My lesson was not long or unusual: one 45-minute lesson. If I hadn't read to the very bottom, I'd have handed in a plan missing a required section.
   **Blocker** for formal lesson plans. Fix: allow enough length for a full template, or if it runs out, finish the remaining headings briefly rather than stopping. Never cut off a required section.

2. **The second try also ran out, on a duplicate answer key.** (26-03-lesson-plan, end)
   Every template section was there this time. But the plan then added an extra "Answer Key" that repeats the expected answers already printed in Independent Practice, and that key was cut off too: "7. 4/6 > 4". The "length limit" warning appears again. It isn't harmful, but the copy I'd print would end mid-line with an error message, and the extra key is a section my template said not to add.
   **Major.** Fix: when a template is pasted in, don't add sections that aren't in it. Don't repeat answers that already appear elsewhere in the document.

3. **The materials don't match the lesson in plan 1.** (26-01-lesson-plan, sections 7 and 8)
   Materials lists "Fraction strips … labeled 1 whole, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/6, 1/8", with no fifths. Yet Direct Instruction says "Put up fraction strips for 2/5 and 4/5". Guided practice uses "1/5 and 1/8", and the practice sheet has "1/3 ___ 1/5" and "3/5 ___ 4/5" with "Use fraction strips if you need help." Students would reach for fifths strips that aren't in the kit. (When I told it our kit in plan 3, it did stick to those strips.)
   **Major.** In an observation, this is exactly the kind of mismatch a supervisor writes up. Fix: check that every fraction or model used in the lesson is in the Materials list.

4. **The objective doesn't match the assessment in plan 3.** (26-03-lesson-plan, Learning Objective and Evaluation Criteria vs. Comparison Sheet)
   The objective and the "Meets objective" criteria both say "at least 4 out of 5 pairs" with "a reason for each answer". The Comparison Sheet used as the summative assessment has **8** items and asks for reasons on only 2 ("Reason for number 3", "Reason for number 8"). My supervisor will check that the objective, the assessment and the criteria line up. They don't.
   **Major.** Fix: make the item count and the criterion count match, and make sure the assessment actually collects the evidence the objective promises.

5. **Plan 3 leaves out the "same whole" part of the standard.** (26-03-lesson-plan, Direct Instruction and Closure)
   Standard 3.NF.A.3.D, which the plan quotes correctly, includes "Recognize that comparisons are valid only when the two fractions refer to the same whole." Plan 1 taught this ("The same whole rule (2 minutes)"). Plan 3 never teaches or checks it. It also states the rule up front ("the fraction with the smaller denominator is greater") before students reason with the strips.
   **Minor to major**, since it's a standards gap in a formal observation. Fix: when the standard's text is quoted, make sure every part of it is taught or assessed.

6. **The site didn't follow my "exact heading names" instruction.** (both lesson plans)
   My template said "with these exact names". Plan 1 renamed some headings: "Learning Objectives" instead of "Learning Objective(s)", "Syntax and Discourse" instead of "Syntax or Discourse", "Students with IEPs (reading)" instead of "Students with IEPs / 504 Plans" (the 504 part was dropped). Plan 3 dropped all the section numbers (1–12) that my template used. My university checks headings against the template.
   **Minor.** Fix: when a template is pasted in, copy its headings and numbering exactly.

7. **A student "Name / Date" line appears at the top of a teacher lesson plan.** (26-01 and 26-03, line 3)
   Both plans start with "**Name:** ______ **Date:** ______", the same as a student worksheet, and then my Candidate Information section repeats name and date. It looks unprofessional in a document I hand to my supervisor.
   **Minor.** Fix: leave the student name line off lesson plans, unit plans and other teacher-facing documents.

8. **The exit ticket's sentence frame breaks on one of its own questions, and the questions are lopsided.** (26-02-exit-ticket)
   The frame is "I know that ___ is ___ than ___ because ___". For question 4 (2/8 vs 2/8, answer =), that frame gives "is equal than", which is ungrammatical. Question 4 compares a fraction with itself, which doesn't really test anything. Only 1 of the 4 items (5/6 vs 5/8) tests same numerator; the other three are same denominator. I also asked for support for my reading IEPs and Spanish-speaking ELLs, and the ticket has none: no Spanish words, no read-aloud note. The answers and the misconception notes were otherwise correct and useful.
   **Minor.** Fix: give a frame that also works for "equal to". Balance same-numerator and same-denominator items. Use the IEP/ELL details the teacher typed in.

9. **There's no guidance on using this for coursework or graded work.** (/about, /terms)
   As a student teacher, I need to know whether I'm allowed to submit an AI-drafted plan and how to disclose it. Nothing on the site mentions that. The Terms say "Documents are written by AI and may contain errors". That's fine, but it doesn't help me.
   **Opinion / minor.** Fix: add a short note along the lines of "If you are a student teacher, check your program's AI policy and disclose use."

Smaller things I noticed:
- /privacy says "Questions or removal requests: the site owner." There's no actual way to contact anyone.
- /privacy and /terms both still show a note to the site owner: "Site owner: review this page with a qualified adviser before launch…". It should be removed before launch.
- The Grade 1 phonics library page's "Handy for this lesson" Amazon links are "Sticky notes for annotating" and "Reading guide strips". They don't really match a first-grade CVC lesson.

## What worked

- **The template option really works.** Both plans used my sections in my order, including the four Academic Language parts (function, vocabulary, syntax/discourse, supports) and Prior Knowledge **and Assets**, which generic generators usually skip. Plan 3 even added the blank Post-Lesson Reflection prompts the way I asked.
- **The math was correct throughout.** I worked every comparison in all three documents (for example 2/8 < 2/6, 4/6 > 4/8, 1/4 < 1/3, 5/6 > 5/8) and found no wrong answers. The standard was quoted word for word correctly.
- **The minutes add up.** Both plans total exactly 45 (5 + 10 + 12 + 13 + 5).
- When I listed our real fraction strips, plan 3 used only those. The Spanish vocabulary (numerador, denominador, mayor que, menor que, igual a) was correct and helpful. The exit ticket's notes on what each wrong answer means, with a quick reteach for each, are the kind of thing my cooperating teacher would like.
- It's free, needs no sign-up, and was fast (7–20 seconds). The library's Grade 1 CVC plan was careful and accurate.

But read it all the way to the bottom: my first plan just stopped before the reflection section. Check that the materials, objective and assessment match each other, because they didn't always.
