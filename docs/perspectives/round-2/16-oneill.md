# Mr. O'Neill, high school assistant principal who runs substitute coverage

**Verdict:** Would use, with conditions. It is fast enough for a 6:30am call, but I would not hand a generated plan to a substitute unread, and right now two of the three I made needed fixing first.

**In one sentence:** The plan arrives in 15 to 19 seconds and the structure is right, but one answer key printed wrong answers and then "corrected" itself on the page, "Full day" gave me about two and a half hours, and one plan depends on an online quiz that does not exist.

## Problems found

### 1. The math answer key prints wrong answers and then corrects itself on the page (major, close to a blocker)
Where: 16-02-sub-plan.md, Answer Key, Practice Set problems 9 and 11.
What I saw: "9. e = -12 (7e + 2 = 3e - 10, so 4e = -12, e = -3)" followed by "Correction: For problem 9, 4e = -12 gives e = -3." Then "11. g = 8 (... so g = 2)" followed by "Corrected answer for problem 11: g = 2." and finally "Note to substitute: Students may find that the answers to problems 9 and 11 above are corrected. Please use these values".
Why it matters: the plan tells the sub "You do not need any math background" and to read answers from the key. A sub reading down the list says "e equals negative twelve" to thirty ninth graders. The right values (-3 and 2) are there, but buried in a mess. Problem 10 is also listed twice.
Fix I want: the key must be checked and cleaned before the document is shown, so that each problem has one answer, with no "Correction" lines ever appearing on the page.

### 2. "Full day" produced about 2 hours 40 minutes, for one class (major)
Where: 16-02-sub-plan.md, "Schedule for the day".
What I saw: the schedule runs from "[start time]" to "[start time + 150]" plus 10 minutes of pack-up, with one warm-up, one practice set, one challenge set and one exit ticket. No lunch, no mention of periods.
Why it matters: in a high school a full day is five to seven periods, usually several sections of the same course. This plan neither fills a day nor says "repeat this for each period". The library's grade 6 full-day plan handles it properly ("The plan fills about five hours" and "If students change classes each period: use one block per class period"). The generated one does not.
Fix I want: for grades 6-12, ask "how many periods and how long?" (or assume it), write one period plan and say clearly "repeat for each section", and include enough extra work for a second section of a different level.

### 3. The Spanish plan depends on an online quiz that does not exist, and the quiz text itself is garbled (major)
Where: 16-03-sub-plan.md, Activity 3: Chromebook Quiz.
What I saw: "Open the quiz called Spanish 1 Present Tense -ar Review. The link is [quiz link or name in the class site]." Question 9 reads "(a) caminan (c) caminamos (b) caminamos", and then a note: "Teacher will fix this in the quiz after the class."
Why it matters: I ticked "Student devices available". The site answered by assuming the sick teacher will build a ten-question online quiz before 7:45. That is 20 minutes of an 85-minute block with nothing behind it. And the document knew question 9 was broken (two identical choices, letters out of order) and left it in with an apology.
Fix I want: when devices are available, either give an activity that needs no pre-built link (students type answers into a blank document, or the quiz is printed on paper as the fallback), or say plainly at the top "this needs a link from the teacher; if none, do this on paper instead". Never print a note saying an error will be fixed later.

### 4. Every plan is full of square-bracket blanks scattered through the text (major for my use)
Where: all three. Examples: 16-01 "Find the teacher's lesson plan folder, [location of lesson folder]", "[attendance sheet location]", "[signal, e.g., raised hand]", "[discipline log location]", "[dismissal procedure]"; 16-02 "Students will be seated in [seating chart location]", "[start time + 75]", "[bell schedule]"; 16-03 "[Chromebook cart location]", "[bathroom and water policy]".
I counted about 8 in the first plan, 11 in the second and 8 in the third.
Why it matters: generating takes 17 seconds, but then a sick teacher has to hunt through three pages for brackets inside sentences. If they miss them, the sub reads "Dismiss students according to [dismissal procedure]". The library plan does this much better with one fill-in table at the top ("Regular teacher: fill in before you leave this in your sub folder").
Fix I want: put all school-specific items in one table at the top, as the library plan does, and keep the body free of brackets ("see the table on page 1"). Better still, let me type bell times, attendance method and office extension into the form once.

### 5. Wrong and off-topic items in the Spanish answer key (major, because the sub cannot catch them)
Where: 16-03-sub-plan.md, Activity 2 items 16 and 18 and their answers.
What I saw: item 18 asks "¿Dónde trabajan ustedes?" and the key begins "Ellos trabajan en..." (the right reply is "Nosotros trabajamos en...", which the key then gives as the sample, so the key contradicts itself). Item 16 is "They play. (jugar)" with answer "Ellos juegan"; jugar is not a regular -ar verb, so it does not belong in a review of regular -ar verbs and students will write "jugan". Also Activity 4 Part C: "Write one sentence about your partner using ellos or ellas" - one partner is not "they".
Fix I want: keep every item inside the stated topic, and give one clear answer per item.

### 6. The Spanish plan says the sub need not speak Spanish, then has the sub lead Spanish aloud (minor)
Where: 16-03-sub-plan.md, Notes and Activity 1.
What I saw: "You do not need to speak Spanish to run this plan" and then "Now I'll say a verb and you say the correct form with me. Yo, ... hablo. Tú, ... hablas" and "have students repeat after you: Estudiar (to study). Yo estudio..."
Fix I want: have a student volunteer lead the choral reading, or have students read it to a partner.

### 7. Instructions do not match the handouts in the chemistry plan (minor)
Where: 16-01-sub-plan.md.
What I saw: the timeline says "work the first two examples together" but there is only one example (H2 + O2). The notes say "students can copy the four problems from the board" but Sheet A has eight problems and Sheet B five more. Students are told to "Work on Part 1 and Part 2" but Part 1 is only the already-answered example. Early finishers are told to "check each other's answers for Part 2 using the answer key", which hands the key to students.
All thirteen chemistry answers are correct; I balanced each one.
Fix I want: a consistency pass so counts in the notes match what is on the sheets.

### 8. Student pages are not always separate from the sub's script (major for a 7:45 photocopy run)
Where: 16-02-sub-plan.md (worst), 16-03-sub-plan.md.
What I saw: in the math plan the 12-problem Practice Set, the Challenge Problems and the Exit Ticket sit inside the sub's read-aloud text with no "Student Page" heading and no name line. The notes never say what to copy. The chemistry plan did this properly ("Student Sheet A", "Student Sheet B"). The math plan also says "put your practice set in the folder on the desk. We will check answers together in a few minutes" - they have just handed it in. And it tells early finishers to check with the Answer Key "(teacher copy only; do not share it with students...)".
Fix I want: every sub plan should open with "What to copy (one per student)" and put each student page on its own sheet, as the library plan does.

### 9. Smaller things
- Every sub plan starts with "Name: ____ Date: ____" directly under the title. That is a student name line on a page meant for the substitute. (minor)
- None of the three generated plans has an end-of-day report for the sub to fill in (absences, what got done, incidents). The library plan has a good one. As the person who reads those notes, I want it on every plan. (minor, missing feature)
- All three scripts say "Good morning", including plans that repeat in the afternoon. (minor)
- The library has exactly one sub plan, for grade 6. Filtering to Sub Plan and grades 9-12 gives "Nothing here yet for that combination." For a high school that is an empty shelf. (missing feature)
- The Terms say "Daily limits apply" but no page says what the limit is. On a bad flu morning I might need eight plans before 7:30; I need to know if I will be cut off. (minor)
- /privacy and /terms still show the note "Site owner: review this page with a qualified adviser before launch, and add your business name and address", and the contact line reads "Questions or removal requests: the site owner." with no way to reach anyone. (must be fixed before launch)

## What worked

- Speed. 15, 17 and 19 seconds. The site's "Takes about 15 seconds" is honest. The form is four fields and I never had to sign in.
- It listened to the optional box. The Spanish plan used my 85-minute blocks exactly, said "This plan is taught twice, once in each 85-minute block", and the chemistry plan fit 50 minutes.
- The tone toward the sub is right: read-aloud scripts in quotation marks, minute-by-minute timelines, and a stock line for questions the sub cannot answer ("I'm not able to help with the Spanish... I'll note your question for your teacher"). That is exactly what a non-specialist needs.
- The chemistry plan (16-01) is close to usable as is: separate student sheets, sensible ramp from easy to hard, all answers correct.
- The library's full-day grade 6 plan is the best thing I saw. One fill-in table, "What to copy", a story with questions, an end-of-day report, and every math puzzle answer checks out (I worked all ten). If the generator produced plans shaped like that one, I would have few complaints.
- Outside the two self-corrected lines, the math in 16-02 is correct: warm-up, worked examples, all other practice answers, challenge problems and exit ticket.

## What I could not check

- How the plans look printed: page breaks between sub notes and student sheets, whether chemical formulas show proper small numbers (the text I saw had plain "H2O").
- The Print, Word, Copy for Google Docs, Edit and Share buttons, and the "Shorter / Easier" revise buttons.
- /my (My documents) only showed "Loading..." through the helper, so I could not see whether a plan made on my office computer can be found again.
- How it behaves on a phone, which is where a sick teacher would actually be at 6:30am.
- What the daily limit is or what happens when it is reached.

## What I would say to a colleague about this site

It gets you a structured sub plan faster than anything else I have tried, and it is free with no login, so keep it bookmarked. But treat what comes out as a draft. Fill the optional box with your period length and the fact that the sub does not know the subject, because it does use that. Then spend five minutes: find every square bracket, work the answer key yourself, and make sure nothing depends on a link or a slide deck you have not made. If you cannot spare those five minutes, print the ready-made plan from the library instead; that one I would hand to a sub as it stands, though it is written for grade 6. I would not let a plan go from this site to a substitute without a teacher or me reading the answer key first.
