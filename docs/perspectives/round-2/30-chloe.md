## Problems found
1. **Lesson handout is missing the parts the lesson tells students to use.** (Lesson plan, Step 4 and Materials.) Step 4 says "Students work on the handout, Part A (card sort) and Part B (cycle diagram)", and Materials says the handout is "the Formative Assessment at the end of this plan". But the Formative Assessment section holds only 4 exit questions. The card-sort statements appear only in the teacher text, with the answers written beside each one, and there is no blank cycle diagram. I would have to build the printable page myself. Severity: major. Fix: add a student handout page that contains the card-sort items without answers and a blank cycle-diagram box, and keep the exit questions on the same page.
2. **Quiz does not match the lesson at the same grade and topic.** (Quiz Q1, Q5, Q7 vs. lesson.) The lesson teaches only the overall equations, the organelles and the cycle. The quiz asks about NADPH, the light-dependent reactions, the Calvin cycle, RuBisCO, photolysis of water, and anaerobic ATP yields. Those are not in the lesson. Students taught from this lesson would fail those questions. Severity: major. Fix: either ask the teacher "is this quiz for the lesson you just made?" or let the quiz tool accept a pasted lesson, and say in the quiz header what level it assumes (for example "AP/advanced").
3. **Grade 10 level is uneven.** (Quiz Q5.) "Which molecule is used to fix carbon dioxide" with the answer RuBisCO, which is an enzyme, not a molecule that is used up. Option B, "ATP only", is an odd distractor. Not wrong, but a student teacher might not catch it. Severity: minor. Fix: reword to "which enzyme".
4. **Q8 scoring guide is muddled.** (Quiz answer key 8.) "Award 1 point for each correctly paired gas-use and gas-production statement, for two valid gases total, or 2 points for a complete explanation." It is hard to tell how to score a half-right answer. Severity: minor. Fix: a simple rubric such as 1 point for the plant's gas use and output, 1 point for the mouse's.
5. **Answer key explanation for Q1 is vague.** "C and D are incorrect or reversed." This does not say why. A new teacher needs the reason. Severity: minor. Fix: one line per wrong option.
6. **Lesson cross-reference is wrong.** (Materials.) "equations in Part 2", but the lesson sections are "Step 2". Small, but confusing at midnight. Severity: minor. Fix: say "Step 2".
7. **Lesson has no total score for the exit questions.** (Formative Assessment scoring guide.) It says 4 points each, but there are no totals or passing mark, and no tie to the learning objectives. Severity: minor.
8. **The "Standards" section is filler when no standard is given.** It says "No standards were specified." A student teacher usually has to show alignment. Severity: minor. Fix: suggest the NGSS HS-LS1-5/HS-LS1-7 codes when the region is the US and no standard is given, flagged as "check against your state".

Science checks that came out right: both balanced equations (6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2 and the reverse), the 180 g glucose to 192 g oxygen calculation, respiration in plant mitochondria, the 2 versus about 30 to 32 ATP comparison, and the lesson timings (they add up to 50).
