# Ms. Iyer, middle school band and choir teacher
**Verdict:** Would use with conditions. The rubric is usable now. The worksheet is not safe to hand out without fixing its answer key. The sub plan needs a lot of filling in.
**In one sentence:** The site does not ignore music completely, but the Art & Music library is empty and the worksheet answer key has real errors, so I would have to check every music document line by line.

## What I did
- Pages visited: home (/), /tools, /library, /library?subject=arts, /library?q=band.
- Looked at the forms for worksheet, sub-plan, rubric and anything.
- Documents generated (3):
  - 09-01-worksheet.md: rhythm worksheet, grade 6, 4/4 time, 10 mixed questions.
  - 09-02-sub-plan.md: band sub plan for a substitute who cannot read music, one class period, no devices.
  - 09-03-rubric.md: concert performance rubric, 4 levels, five criteria I named.
- I did not generate a concert reflection as a separate document. The sub plan did include reflection prompts.

## Problems found
1. **Worksheet answer key contradicts itself (Q4). Blocker.**
   - Where: worksheet Q4 answer.
   - What I saw: "No. Half note (2) + quarter (1) + quarter (1) = 4 beats. Yes, it is complete. (Correct answer: yes...)"
   - Fix: The key should say only "Yes, 2 + 1 + 1 = 4." A student or sub reading it would be confused.
2. **Worksheet answer key has the AI's draft working left in (Q8 and Q10). Major.**
   - Q8 key: "b is actually correct... so check the list again..." It ends on the right answer, c, but reads like scratch work.
   - Q10 key: "Beats: ... = 4 would need checking; a correct version is..."
   - Fix: Check the sums before output and print only the final clean answer.
3. **Syllable table is wrong or ambiguous. Major.**
   - Where: reference table.
   - What I saw: "Eighth note | ti-ti | 1/2". Ti-ti is a PAIR of eighths and equals 1 beat. A single eighth is "ti".
   - Fix: Eighth note = "ti" (1/2 beat). Pair = "ti-ti" (1 beat).
   - Knock-on effect: this makes Q7 and Q10 arithmetic unreliable. In Q10 the key's measure 1, "ta-ta | ti-ti | ti-ti | quarter rest", counts as 2+1/2+1/2+1 = 4, but by the standard meaning of ti-ti it is 5 beats.
   - Also, "whole note = ta-ta-ta-ta (held)" and "half note = ta-ta (held)" look the same as written quarter notes. Students will be confused.
2b. **Notation limits. Minor, expected.** The worksheet did say "printed without a staff" and told students to imagine one. That was honest. There are no rhythm blocks, note symbols, or "write the counts under the beats" grid. For middle school rhythm work, this is the main reason I would not use the worksheet as is.
3. **The Art & Music library is empty. Major (for me).**
   - Where: /library?subject=arts.
   - What I saw: "Nothing here yet for that combination." Searching "band" also shows no music results.
   - Fix: Seed the library with at least a few music items (rhythm worksheet, concert reflection, band sub plan, performance rubric). The "Make one now" link is good, but it shows that arts teachers were not thought of when the library was built.
4. **Sub plan is full of unfilled brackets. Major.**
   - What I saw: "[student leader name...]", "[case storage location]", "[piece title]", "[office extension or number]", about ten in all.
   - Fix: Gather these in a short "fill these in before you print" box at the top, or add form fields for them. As is, a sub could be handed a sheet with brackets in it.
5. **Sub plan is band only and the time doesn't fit the request. Minor to major.**
   - I wrote "band" so band is fair. The tool does not mention choir, general music or mixed ensembles.
   - The plan says Part A is 30 minutes, but I picked "one class period". The timetable ends at 0:30, so a 45-50 minute period has 15-20 minutes unplanned. Part B is "optional" and not scheduled.
   - Fix: Ask for the period length, or schedule the listening/reflection into the plan.
6. **Sub plan has risky or muddled advice for a non-musician. Minor.**
   - "Never allow students to blow into another student's instrument" is good. "Do not let students handle reeds, mouthpieces, or cleaning rods for another person's instrument" is fine.
   - "Ask each section to play a concert B-flat" is a fair thing to ask of beginning band students. A non-musician sub will not know what that means, and the plan does not say to skip it. It did offer "or the note your teacher has written on the board", which helps.
   - The sub-plan tells students to "tell the band teacher in the next note" about a broken instrument. A sub cannot do that. It should say to leave a note for the teacher.
7. **Rubric grade conversion is not scaled sensibly. Minor.**
   - What I saw: "18 to 20 = A... below 12 = Needs support." It says "optional", so fine. The five-criteria table is realistic and uses real music terms (intonation, articulations, pp to ff).
   - It says "Enters and exits..." under "Stage presence" in all four levels. That is usual. It mixes "instrument or voice part" and "performer", which is acceptable for band and choir.
   - Fix: Offer a separate band version and choir version. In the level-4 box, "pp to ff" is too much to expect from many grade 7 groups. Add a note that the teacher should adjust to the dynamics actually in the piece.

## What worked
- The rubric is the best of the three. The language is musical and specific ("rushed dotted rhythm", "air leaks, cracks or squeaks"), and it includes a student self-check. I could use it almost as is.
- The sub plan understood the job: a student leader, a script the sub reads aloud, no need to correct the music, and instrument safety. That is better than I expected.
- The worksheet was honest that it had no staff, and the worked example explains beat counting correctly.
- No sign-up, no names collected, simple tool list. I also liked that arts is a subject choice at all.

## What I could not check
- Print layout, how it looks on a phone, the Print, Word and Copy for Google Docs buttons, and whether any notation appears. I only saw text.
- I could not tell whether the site could draw rhythm notation, staves or note symbols at all. In text it cannot.
- I did not read the privacy or terms pages in full for this review.

## What I would say to a colleague about this site
It is free and quick, and it handled "band sub plan" and "concert rubric" better than most tools I have tried. But the library has nothing for music, and the rhythm worksheet had a wrong table and a messy answer key. If you try it, check the arithmetic yourself, and do not expect anything with actual notation. Use it for rubrics, reflections and sub plans, not for theory worksheets.
