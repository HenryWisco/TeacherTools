# Ms. Begay, teacher of a combined 5th/6th grade class in a small rural school

**Verdict:** Would use, with conditions. The answer keys need cleaning up, and it needs a way to save paper.

**In one sentence:** It listened when I asked for a rural setting and got nearly all the math right. But every answer key I got had the computer "thinking out loud" in it, the leveled versions print the same passage three times, and nothing on the site is set up for a combined-grade class or a page quota.

## What I did

- Pages visited: home page, /tools, /library (with a search for "combined"), one library resource (Ratios and Unit Rates Worksheet, Grade 6), /about, /my.
- Looked at the forms for Worksheet, Math Word Problems, Reading Passage, Exit Ticket and Ask for Anything.
- Generated 3 documents:
  1. `11-01-word-problems.md`: Math Word Problems, multiplying and dividing decimals, 8 problems, three levels, rural theme (hauling water, sheep, school bus, county fair, trading post). I told it the class is combined 5th/6th and asked for one sheet front and back per level, black and white.
  2. `11-02-reading-passage.md`: Reading Passage, nonfiction, "about 300 words," three levels, on families in a high-desert community hauling and saving water. I asked for the same questions at all three levels and one printed page per level.
  3. `11-03-anything.md`: Ask for Anything, a 45-minute fractions lesson for the whole class at once. One shared warm-up, then one student page with a 5th grade half (adding and subtracting unlike denominators) and a 6th grade half (dividing fractions), plus a key. Rural setting, no shading or clip art.

## Problems found

1. **The answer keys contain the AI correcting itself mid-sentence.** (Major. This turned up in all three documents.)
   - Word problems, Level 1 key, #8: "One round trip: 84.6 x 2 = 169.2 miles. Hmm, the question asks for 2 round trips..."
   - Fractions lesson key, Part A: "Hmm, correction for item 5: the amount used is..." This comes right after a line that already gave the answer.
   - Reading passage, Level 3 key, #3: "24 cups per day (3 quarts, or 6 cups = 1.5 quarts ... precisely, 24 cups is 6 quarts)". The final figure is right (24 cups = 6 quarts), but the 3 quarts and 1.5 quarts before it are wrong.
   - Why it matters: I can't hand this key to an aide or a sub, or post it, without retyping it, and it makes me trust the whole page less.
   - Fix I'd want: the key should state each answer once, cleanly. Check keys for "Hmm," "correction," "..." and conflicting numbers before showing the document.

2. **The leveled reading passage prints the same passage three times.** (Major for anyone with a page quota.)
   - The passage in Levels 1, 2 and 3 is word-for-word the same text, and it is printed in full under each level.
   - So "three levels" here really means three question sets, plus two extra copies of a passage I could have printed once. For my class that is wasted pages every time.
   - Fix I'd want: when the passage doesn't change between levels, print it once, followed by the three question sets. If levels are meant to change the reading level, then actually rewrite the passage for each.

3. **The reading passage ignored two of my instructions.** (Major.)
   - Length: I chose "About 300 words." I counted the passage at 192 words.
   - Questions: I asked for "same questions for all three levels so we can discuss together." I got three completely different question sets and one identical passage, which is the opposite of what I asked for.
   - Fix I'd want: keep the length within about 10% of the choice, and follow the "Anything else" box when it conflicts with the default level layout.

4. **No way to say "combined grade."** (Major for multi-grade and rural teachers.)
   - Every form has a single Grade choice ("Grade 5" or "Grade 6"). There is no "5–6" or "multi-grade" option.
   - The library bands split my class in half: "3–5" and "6–8." Searching the library for "combined" gives "Nothing here yet."
   - I had to explain the combined class in the free-text box every time. When I did, the site handled it reasonably: the fractions lesson printed "Combined 5th and 6th grade."
   - Fix I'd want: let me pick a grade range (for example 5 to 6), and offer "one page, one half per grade" as a version choice next to "Three levels."

5. **The word-problem "levels" barely differ, and nothing says which level is for which grade.** (Major.)
   - Level 2 is nearly the same 8 problems as Level 1, with the same numbers (2.5 x 4, 65.5 x 3, 6.4 x 5, 0.75 x 6...). The only differences are that the hints are removed and one multi-step problem is added.
   - The top of the student page says "Fifth grade: 5.NBT.B.7. Sixth grade: 6.NS.B.3. Work through the same content at your level." Standard codes don't belong on a student handout, and the page never connects the levels to the grades.
   - Fix I'd want: Level 1 should use easier numbers, not just added hints. Put teacher notes such as standards and level-to-grade suggestions on a teacher page, not the student page.

6. **Some rural details are unrealistic or confusing.** (Minor.)
   - Word problems:
     - "Each trip brings 2.5 gallons." Nobody hauls 2.5 gallons in a truck. Families here haul 50 to 300 gallons at a time, and my students would laugh.
     - "Each tank holds 0.8 gallon" for watering animals.
     - Level 2 #1: "hauls water in a 150-gallon tank, but each trip uses 2.5 gallons" is muddled.
     - Level 1 #8 says the family drives "84.6 miles to town and back," then tells students to "First find the miles in one round trip," which is already given. That sends kids the wrong way.
   - Reading passage: it says most homes have no water pipes, then says families "take short showers" and "fix leaks right away." That doesn't hang together for a home that hauls its water.
   - The trading post I asked for was never used.
   - Fix I'd want: when the user gives a rural theme, use realistic quantities, and make the details agree with each other.

7. **The "lesson" was really a worksheet, with teacher notes mixed into the student page.** (Minor to major.)
   - I asked for a 45-minute lesson I could teach to everyone at once, and "ONE page per student."
   - I got:
     - a 5-minute warm-up plus two 15-minute practice sections, which is 35 minutes
     - no plan for how to teach two grades at once (who I work with first, what the other group does meanwhile)
     - no closing or check
   - The "Name / Date" line sits under the standards and above "Teacher directions: Write these on the board before students arrive." If I print it as is, students get the teacher notes too.
   - Fix I'd want: separate the teacher plan from the student page, so I can print just the student page. For a combined class, include a short "who does what when" schedule.

8. **Inconsistent name lines.** (Minor.)
   - The leveled reading passage has one Name/Date line at the very top, so the Level 2 and Level 3 pages have no name line.
   - The word problems put a name line on each level, plus an extra one at the top.
   - Fix I'd want: one name line at the top of each level, because each level goes to different students.

9. **No paper-saving or ink-saving option.** (Minor, but it matters to me.)
   - The buttons I could see were Print / save PDF, Word, Copy for Google Docs, Edit, Large print and Share. There is "Large print" but no "compact" or "save paper" print.
   - The "Shorter" button might help, but I couldn't test it.
   - Fix I'd want: a "Save paper" option with smaller margins, two half-page exit tickets per sheet, and the key optional or compact.

## What worked

- **It took my local setting seriously.** Sheep, goats, hay bales, fence posts, fry bread, stew, hauling water, cisterns and the school bus route all showed up. Fry bread and stew were treated as ordinary family food, not as something exotic. It didn't use subways or malls. I could steer the content with the "Anything else" box and the Theme box.
- **The math was right.** I worked every problem in the word problems and the fractions page:
  - Word problems: all 25 answers correct (for example 10 / 0.8 gives 12 full tanks; 27 / 12.5 gives 3 trips; 18 / 2.4 = 7.5 yards; 169.2 / 18 = 9.4 gallons).
  - Fractions page: all 13 answers correct (warm-up plus parts A and B).
  - The reading-passage multiple choice answers were also right.
  - The only errors were the self-corrections in the keys described above.
- **The pages are plain.** Everything came out as plain text with lines to write on, which should print fine in black and white. Generation took 8 to 18 seconds, about what the home page promises.
- **The library resource was solid.** The Grade 6 Ratios and Unit Rates worksheet I checked was clean, with a correct, well-explained key.
- **No account needed.** The About page is straightforward that AI can make mistakes. Documents are saved only in my browser, which suits a shared school laptop.

## What I could not check

- How heavy the pages are on slow internet, and whether the site works on an old laptop. The helper only shows text, not download size or speed.
- What the printout actually looks like: page count, margins, whether anything prints in gray or color.
- What the Print, Word, Large print, Shorter, Edit and "Want it different?" buttons actually do.
- Whether /my works. It says "This page needs JavaScript switched on," which I couldn't run.
- How it looks on a phone.

## What I would say to a colleague about this site

"It's free, there's no sign-up, and if you tell it you're out here with sheep and water hauling, it actually writes problems about that. The math checked out for me. But read the answer key before you use it, because it sometimes leaves in the AI's 'Hmm, correction...' notes. And watch the leveled versions: the reading one printed the same passage three times, which will burn through your copy quota. For a combined class you have to spell out the two grades in the box yourself."
